About Le Kashmir

Le Kashmir is an Indian and Pakistani restaurant located in the heart of Renens. You can enjoy typical dishes cooked in the tandoor (traditional Indian oven), vegetables, poultry, lamb or seafood with special mention for Kofta curry (meatballs with lemon sauce, ginger and coriander) and aubergine fritters. The service is very friendly, provided by a quality staff. Great selection of wines from Switzerland, France, Italy, California, Spain, Argentina, Chile and India.
