    The rooms

    Just above the restaurant, in the facility of the (prison)hotel Barabas, three new and cosy rooms with Japanese decor guarantee a restful residence. Make yourself comfortable in 20 square meters, enjoy the beautiful interior design and explore Lucerne’s old town on your own or just get some well-deserved sleep.
