Real wood-fired pizza

Each of our pizzas is prepared with passion and hand-picked ingredients before being baked in a real wood-fired oven. This traditional cooking method ignites the aroma, creates an irresistibly crispy crust and gives every bite an incomparable depth of flavor. Experience the difference a wood-burning stove makes!

Tagliata di Manzo

Tagliata di Manzo is an Italian steak dish known for its simplicity and flavor. It consists of grilled beef steaks cut into slices and served on rocket with parmesan, olive oil and balsamic vinegar. This dish combines tender meat with fresh, slightly bitter rocket and is a classic of Italian cuisine.
