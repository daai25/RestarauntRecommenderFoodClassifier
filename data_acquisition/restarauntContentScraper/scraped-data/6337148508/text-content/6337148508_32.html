Sustainability @ Hotel Adler Zürich

Our measures at a glance

For the environment. For us all.

As an independent hotel, located in the heart of Zurich’s historic city center, reducing our footprint always has been a top priority for us. We truly believe that many smaller measures impact the big picture, and are glad when our guests appreciate and support these.

Power Optimization System

We continuously analyze and optimize our electricity/energy usage.

Waste Separation and Recycling

Efficient separation of waste and recycling of re-usable materials.

Shower gel/Shampoo in big dispensers

Whenever possible, we reduce the use of plastic, particularly single-use plastic.

Partner of EWZ Energy

Partner of the city’s power supplier (EWZ) to optimize and reduce electricity usage.

Employee Trainings

Internal and external training for our whole staff, enable us to reduce our footprint significantly.

Change of Towels on-demand

Help us to save energy by changing towels on-demand (less washing).

LED-Blubs only

All our lamps are equipped with energy-saving and long-lasting LED-bulbs.
