Central. Inspiring. Modern.

For conferences, sessions or creative meetings, hire the historic antiquarian bookshop meeting room in the center of the old town of Zurich, with window frontage on the Rosengasse. At 18 square meters, up to 6 persons can sit at the meeting table and up to 12 persons with theatre-seating.

Combine your business event with a business lunch in our own Swiss Chuchi restaurant, a guided tour of the city or a visit to the Zurich Art Museum.

The antiquarian bookshop meeting room is a really inspirational location: On the one hand, the guest house once sheltered in its stalls the cows and horses which inspired Rudolf Koller to paint, when he was still a child. On the other hand, later on there was much to read, as the Nietlisbach family ran their antiquarian bookshop here, called “Zum Guten Buch”.

Important: to book our meeting room, a hotel room reservation is required.
