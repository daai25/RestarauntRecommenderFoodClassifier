Swiss Specialities in the Heart of Zurich

Whether in the open air of the bustling Niederdorf pedestrian precinct or in the quaint Swiss ambience of the restaurant: Enjoy what your heart desires – from the Adler Special fondue, through Raclette to the Waadtländer fondue. From Alp-herder’s macaroni through Capuns to Zurich ragout with rosti.

From the first fondue parlour in the old town of Zurich to the number one address for Swiss cheese and meat specialities. The Swiss Chuchi restaurant attracts both locals and people from far and wide who appreciate good food.

Review and follow us

     

Traditional. Typical. Value for money.​

No matter if you want to try the famous cheese fondue (we offer 9 different variations), a melting Raclette, or another Swiss specialty such as the Zurich-style veal fricassée, we are the place to be for lovers of the Swiss cuisine.

  • Serving Swiss dishes since 1953
  • Daily lunch specials for less than CHF 20
  • Lots of desserts including chocolate fondue
  • Kids menus as from CHF 9.50
  • Breakfast buffet for non-hotel-guests: CHF 20
  • We serve water for dogs and even offer them a stand-up bar outside

Groups

We welcome groups of up to 8 people. A predefined and tailored menu possible, or guests choose from the Menu à la carte. Please send your inquiry to info@swiss-chuchi.ch.

Breakfast for Non-Hotel-Guests

We offer non-hotel-guests a varied breakfast buffet including homemade muesli, cheese, cold cuts, pastry, and different sorts of bread. Breakfast is served during the week from 6:30 until 10:00 and at weekends from 6:30 until 10:30.

Price: CHF 20/per person
