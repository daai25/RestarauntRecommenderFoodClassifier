Food made for pleasure

From our old family recipes, the menu has been reimagined and we strive to give every meal the effort it deserves. The diversity of Chinese, Cambodian, Thai and Vietnamese food can make you feel like traveling through Asia. By making every sauce in the restaurant and the use of fresh ingredients and Swiss meat, every menu is being cooked “à la minute”.

1987

A place with a story

Lemongrass is through and through a historical restaurant. It symbolically stands in the same place where the story of Suan Long started in 1987.

The restaurant stands for traditional food that has been reimagined and constantly evolved over the last decades. A small and private place, Lemongrass is a cozy and homely place which always gives full attention to our guest.
