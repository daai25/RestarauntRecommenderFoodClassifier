Restaurant
Alpiglen

The Swiss Cheese Toasts at Alpiglen are legendary

In our restaurant we offer tasty local specialities and traditional Swiss cuisine. The restaurant can seat up to 50 people. Whether you are interested in a casual gathering, a formal dinner, a family meeting or a cheese fondue party in wintertime, we are happy to meet your requests.

Menu:

  • Legendary Swiss cheese toast
  • Cheese Fondue
  • Apple Fritters
  • Home made pies
  • Cold dishes with a range of local specialities (Alpine cheese, air–cured meat, bacon, sausages)
  • Variations of hash brown potatoes

Our Hit:
