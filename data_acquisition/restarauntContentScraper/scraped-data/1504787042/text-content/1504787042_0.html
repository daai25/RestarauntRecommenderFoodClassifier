SERVING UNIQUE CULTURAL EXPERIENCES AND THE VIBRANT FLAVOURS OF INDIA.

Nestled in the heart of the Swiss Alps, GourmIndia has been a cherished part of the community since its establishment in 2007. A culinary gem born from a relentless pursuit of perfection, our restaurant has seamlessly blended the warmth of Indian culinary traditions with Swiss sophistication, becoming a staple in the hearts of our patrons.

 

Every dish on our menu is a carefully composed, creating a symphony of Indian flavours that transcends the ordinary. Join us on a gastronomic voyage where tradition meets innovation, offering a diverse range of regional Indian specialties that redefine fine dining.

ELEVATING DINING TO AN ART FORM

At GourmIndia, immerse yourself in the luxurious ambiance that surrounds you, complemented by the renowned hospitality of India. Experience fine dining like never before, where each meal is a standing ovation to the fusion of tradition and innovation, creating memories that linger long after the last bite.
