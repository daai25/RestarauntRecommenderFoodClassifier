    With AlpäTraum, a quaint and cozy delicatessen at Promenade 144 in Davos Dorf, Marianne Müller upholds the alpine tradition with specialties from the region and beyond.

     

    In addition to high-quality, regional specialties, Marianne has added homemade jams, fine wines, beverages, pastas, sauces, sausages, and many other delicacies to the shelves. You’ll also find the best cheese fondue all-in-one mixture in AlpäTraum.

     

    Homemade cookies, cakes, breads, soups, and more round off her tasty palette of products, also as take away.

     

    Summer or winter, you can drop in for a coffee or a glass of wine or prosecco and enjoy warm soups, delicious, freshly-made sandwiches, or cold meat and cheese platters. On sunny days the terrasse is open.

     

    You can create a delicious gift basket or gift bag for colleagues, friends, family, birthdays, anniversaries, weddings etc. Marianne and her team will happily help you make a selection according to your individual wishes and ideas.

     

    Stop by and take a closer look at the wonderful selection of products…you won’t want to go home empty-handed.
