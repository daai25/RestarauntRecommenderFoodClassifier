Our food as authentic and fresh can be taken out.

The Lebanese Cuisine

The Lebanese cuisine is internationally renowned for its versatility and sensuality and belongs to the oldest in the world. It is part of the Mediterranean cuisine and is mainly based on fresh vegetables. It includes an abundance of fresh seasonal ingredients, which are prepared and seasoned with herbs and spices creating an assortment of dishes and combinations which are almost limitless.

Lebanese cuisine is light and is therefore in line with today’s trend for a modern and health-conscious diet.
