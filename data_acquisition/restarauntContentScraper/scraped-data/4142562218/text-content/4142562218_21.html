Welcome

Welcome to Restaurant Alter Tobelhof – a place where time slows down, history comes alive, and Swiss cuisine is celebrated in its most authentic form. Nestled in a heritage-listed farmhouse with roots dating back to 1315, our restaurant offers more than just a meal – it offers an experience steeped in tradition and Swiss hospitality. At Restaurant Alter Tobelhof, we serve honest, down-to-earth dishes prepared with natural ingredients – just the way they’ve always been. No elaborate reinterpretations, no frills – just familiar flavors that bring back memories and create new ones.


For over 60 years, Restaurant Alter Tobelhof has been a beloved destination for locals and visitors alike – a place where generations gather, stories are shared, and guests feel right at home. Many who once came here as children with their parents or grandparents now return with children of their own. We believe in connecting generations – through food, atmosphere, and a sense of belonging.
