The buffet

Thai au Lac Restaurant’s soup and appetizer menu includes traditional and authentic dishes, such as spicy seafood soups, sweet coconut milk and chicken soups, as well as various crispy and fresh appetizers, such as spring rolls and dumplings. Each dish is designed to offer a taste of the richness of Thai cuisine, with fresh ingredients and flavors that perfectly balance spice and sweetness.
