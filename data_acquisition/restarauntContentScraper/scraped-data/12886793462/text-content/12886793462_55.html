PÖSTLI Grill

The Pöstli GRILL, with our lively show kitchen, is the place to be for steak lovers; meat is the main focus here. A living room atmosphere prevails; making guests feel at home, visit after visit. A range of organic, vegetarian friendly or gluten-free options is also available for guests with special requests.
