Event Rooms

The options for events are almost limitless. We pride ourselves on meeting your exact requirements,
whether for a small board meeting or a formal banquet, an evening out with close friends or a once-in-a-lifetime wedding.

 

MOROSANI SCHWEIZERHOF - EXCLUSIVE:

Benefit from the exclusivity of the MOROSANI SCHWEIZERHOF in summer! Incentives of more than 80 rooms can book the hotel exclusively and take advantage of the entire conference premises (413 m² plenary room and 5-7 breakout rooms). Meet in a private atmosphere and enjoy our attentive service. Everything focuses on you and your needs.

The Gardenhall foyer can be connected to become a ballroom ensemble: suitable for formal or informal receptions.

Fact Sheet La Cave

La Cave is a tastefully and charmingly decorated "Fonduestube"/ restaurant, where joie de vivre is experienced. With décor evoking a beautiful chalet in the Alps, La Cave offers a cosy atmosphere for dinners, gathering and cocktail receptions up to 100 people (seated) with night caps or parties in the Pöstl Club. The doors of its vault can be closed to separate the vault from the main restaurant to create intimacy for private events of up to 40 people.
