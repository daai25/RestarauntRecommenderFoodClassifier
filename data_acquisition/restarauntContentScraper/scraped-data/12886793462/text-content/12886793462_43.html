Summer Take 4-7

Unlike traditional half board packages, the Dine Around package offers a meal (lunch or dinner can be freely chosen daily) which guests can decide to enjoy in any of the wonderful restaurants in our hotels. This is just another example of the “freedom is luxury” philosophy at the Morosani Hotels Davos.

WHAT'S INCLUDED:

  • Rich breakfast 
  • 3- course dinner or lunch at the restaurant of your choice
  • Complimentary newspaper application for our hotel Android device
  • Gratis WIFI

Tourist taxes are not included. A minimum stay of 4 nights applies.
