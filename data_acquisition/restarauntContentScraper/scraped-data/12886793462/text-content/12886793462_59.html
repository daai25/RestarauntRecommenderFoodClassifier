Morosani Posthotel

The "PÖSTLI", as it is affectionately called by our regular guests, is one of the very best addresses in the heart of Davos.

Here the nights are LONGER, the days are BRIGHTER, dreams are CLOSER and the heart beats FASTER.

This historic building has plenty of space for eating, drinking and meeting, with 90 bedrooms, an indoor swimming pool and sauna area, 4 restaurants, and the city's best lounge and night club.
