A grand choice of restaurants

What kind of cuisine do you feel like enjoying today?

The traditional Swiss Cuisine, the Alta Cucina Italiana, BBQ Specialties from our show kitchen or a French Cuisine with 14 GaultMillau points? The culinary repertoire at the Morosani Hotels Davos is simply too good to not fully take advantage of it.

 

MHD DINE AROUND

The Dine Around is bookable for hotel guests with a bed & breakfast room rate and as from 3 nights: CHF 65 per adult and night / CHF 35 per child and night (5-11 years old) / CHF 65 per child per night (from 12 years). This offer can only be booked during the entire stay (except Christmas / New Year period). The following options are available for our hotel guests with the MOROSANI Dine Around:

  • Pamper yourself in the evening with the half board programme with a 3 course Menu in the restaurant of your choice. Please note that the half board supplement (Dine Around) does not include beverages.

  • Individually order "à la carte" and discover all our restaurants. At the end of your sojourn, a maximum of CHF 65 per adult will be credited to your room bill. Beverages and other extras will be charged separately.

  • Instead of having dinner at the hotel, you can enjoy  a lunch or snacks with coffee and cake from our own patisserie in one of our hotel bars.

MOROSANI Restaurants & Bars

The Dine Around Programme means that you can eat out every night at a different Morosani Restaurant. The Dine Around concept leads you right across all cuisines: a choice of 6 restaurants and 2 hotel bars with different specialties and ambiences. Choose your favourite Morosani restaurant, or one after the other...

swiss and international evergreens in the Pöstli Restaurant:

You will be enchanted by the Grisons atmosphere. Home-style cooking promises an enjoyable evening with soft music. 

Coziness at the Pöstli Grill (Show Kitchen):

The atmosphere is relaxed; it has something of comfort and international nonchalance. Anyone who dines and drinks here will be celebrating at the same time, because the Pöstli Grill is open till late.

Fondue in La Cave & Pöstli Club (THU-Sun):

Make the most of your evening with a visit to the Pöstli Club. Experience dancing hours that really crown the night's adventure. On weekends you can dance until your legs can no longer carry you.

Alta Cucina Italiana in DAMORO:

DAMORO è un luogo unico dove possono assaporare una cena inspirata alla vera tradizione siciliana. It tutto immerso in un'atmosfera esclusiva che regala un'esperienza sensoriale completa e unica. The Soirée begins with antipasti and ends with a real Italian grappa. 

Pöstli Corner Bar & Take Away:

The Pöstli Corner enriches the legendary culinary diversity of MOROSANI restaurants. You can have the MHD-Lunch Around and enjoy light, fresh cuisine. You can have lunch at the restaurant or take it home / to your room. In the evening or at lunchtime, you can order our daily specials à la carte or delight yourself with our Bowl specialties.

DAMIA PIZZA:

Part of the Morosani restaurant Family, DAMIA, has a relaxed vibe and serve pizzas, cicchetti and homemade pasta anlogside negronis on tap, cocktails and wine. You can have lunch at the restaurant or take it home / to your room. 

Extra Benefits

PÖSTLI Lounge / Bolero Bar:

Please note that we do not grant any credit for dinners outside the Morosani Restaurants. However, in the afternoon you can savour our lunches or snacks with coffee and cake from our own patisserie at the hotel bar of your choice. If you order lunch and do not dine with us on the same evening, we will grant you the half board-credit.

Reservations & Policies

Bookable only during the entire stay - as from two consecutive room nights.
Half board-Credit: Please note that we do not grant any credit for dinners outside the Morosani Restaurants.

Table reservations
We recommend reserving a table prior to your arrival. Late dining reservations are subject to availability. We will gladly advise you in the selection of a restaurant and take care of the table reservation on your behalf. Please be advised that the availability of the restaurants is subject to the seasonal opening hours. The Dine Around offer is limited during the festive holidays (Christmas and New Year).

Exceptions
Gala and special events. Detailed information is available at the Front Desk.
