FONDUEKELLER LA CAVE

"La Cave" is a tastefully and charmingly decorated "Fonduestube"/ restaurant, where joie de vivre is experienced. Serving rich and velvety fondue in a traditional Swiss environment, it is great for a casual night out in a friendly atmosphere.

For those who like to spend long nights with friends or who simply enjoy a social get-together at night, the “Pöstli Club ” is just next door.
