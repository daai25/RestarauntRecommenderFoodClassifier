PÖSTLI  Lounge

We are no stranger to traditional innovation. The Pöstli Lounge was refurbished in 2016; the hotel's old furniture was upholstered with lively fabrics, the walls covered with theatrical design and the wooden panels polished. The Lounge is an informal place for eating, drinking and meeting.

Our guests can feel our history, the years of incredible stories and the lives that have touched our location. Now there is laughter, dancing and deep exchanges into the early mornings.
