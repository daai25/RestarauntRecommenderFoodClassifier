    By Public Transport

    From Zurich – Main Station. From the Zurich HB by either of these tram lines 6, 7 or 13, one will arrive at the tram station stockerstrasse , which is conveniently located in front of the street and located adjacent lane.

    By plane

    Trains run regularly between the Zurich airport and Zurich main station, with a journey time of less than 15 mins. 

    By car

    Our restaurant is located in a traffic calmed zone, which is displayed as impassable on specific navigation devices. However access for restaurant is permitted. We recommend planning the route with Google maps for the most accurate directions.
