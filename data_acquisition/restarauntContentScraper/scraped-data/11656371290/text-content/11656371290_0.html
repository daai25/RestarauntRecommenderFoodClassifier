    The Legend of Kailash Parbat

    Started in 1950s by Mulchandani brothers, Kailash Parbat is one of the most iconic brand in Indian restaurant space for ethnic, vegetarian North Indian and Sindhi cuisine. As a group Kailash Parbat operates 70 units including other brands. The brand is well known for being focused on customer delight over its 70+ year legacy, becoming synonymous with North Indian delicacies especially chaats & gravies.
