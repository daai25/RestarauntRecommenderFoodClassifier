Where we are

Located in the quiet area of Locarno, a 5-minute walk from Piazza Grande, Ristorante Stella offers simply furnished rooms at reasonable prices, and free Wi-Fi.

Lake Maggiore is a 10-minute walk away. In addition, Ristorante Stella is close to Piazza Castello, a lively venue during the Locarno Film Festival.

The restaurant serves aromatic Italian cuisine, including 30 types of pizza. The dishes are prepared with care, possibly using local ingredients.

Being located on the highest floors, the accommodations at Ristorante Stella guarantee a peaceful night's sleep. They are accessible by lift and equipped with air conditioning or a fan.

This area of Locarno is one of our guests' favourites, according to independent reviews.

The couples appreciate the location very much: they rated it 8.2 for a trip for two.
