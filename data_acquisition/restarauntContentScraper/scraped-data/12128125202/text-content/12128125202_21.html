The BISTRO / CATERING
at KULTURHAUS VILLA STRÄULI

"My credo is 'Connecting food and people'. The Kulturhaus Villa Sträuli offers to a broad public the possibility for encounters, art and cultural events".

– Luca Verardi

The Bistro of Villa Sträuli is truly an oasis in the middle of Winterthur and combines culture and culinary delights. We offer seasonal as well as varied menus to amaze you. You can expect daily changing creations with a certain touch of the world. But you are also at the right place if you just want to enjoy a coffee. The bistro is open every weekday for lunch. During the summer months, the atmospheric garden is also open to accommodate you.

The Bistro can also be integrated into your event and provide catering for your event. Our goal is for our guests to feel inspired after their event. Your host Luca Verardi and his team will devise customized creations adapted to your needs.
