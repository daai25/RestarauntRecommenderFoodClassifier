Since 1890 the Perozzi family has owned and operated the Alpino Restaurant in Sonogno. Traditions are most important to us, from the use of local products to the guests’ comforts. The restaurant has three dining rooms that can accommodate up to 70 people, and a terrace for 100 people. Any group size is more than welcome. We have 9 bedrooms with 20 beds that are up to date and meet current standards.

Beautiful Mountain Views

The source of the Verzasca River is on the peaks above Sonogno from where it follows the namesake valley, down to the Corippo fork and flows into the artificial lake. The clear emerald-colored waters and the pristine nature that surrounds it make it an appreciated destination in summer.
