Our french Menu

The real French brasserie experience

You’ll always find traditional French cuisine at Brasserie Lipp Restaurant. Using carefully selected products, the Lipp kitchen team prepares each dish with passion. Enjoy the classics, such as sauerkraut with sausage, as well as the freshest seafood and oysters available. Our experienced staff will help you with your menu choices and recommend the perfect wine to complement your meal.

RESTAURANT MENU

WINE SELECTION

 

Whether for lunch, petit-déjeuner or a coffee, the Brasserie with its restaurant and large summer terrace is one of the most beautiful locations on Bahnhofstrasse. The Brasserie Lipp is just the right place for connoisseurs and connoisseurs who appreciate the unique Parisian flair – and that in the middle of Zurich.
