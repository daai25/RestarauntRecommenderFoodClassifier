RISTORANTE ORNELLAIA

Ristorante Ornellaia is a joint venture between the top Tuscan winery and the Bindella family of Zurich. Ornellaia was founded in 1981 in the famous wine village of Bolgheri. Bindella has been importing the prestigious Tenuta's wines to Switzerland since the very beginning - and has been cultivating upscale Italianità for several generations as a gastronomy company and wine shop.

In the immediate vicinity of Zurich's Bahnhofstrasse, probably one of the most exclusive addresses internationally, Ornellaia and Bindella celebrate their expertise in wine and hospitality in the joint Ristorante Ornellaia, framed by impressions of the breathtakingly beautiful landscapes around Bolgheri.

The Ristorante reflects Ornellaia's dynamic spirit: the wine list includes rarities and older vintages from the "Ornellaia Archivio Storico" as well as large-format bottles that can usually only be purchased at international auctions. The offer is completed with an exclusive selection of wines from Bindella's Tenuta Vallocaia in Montepulciano.
