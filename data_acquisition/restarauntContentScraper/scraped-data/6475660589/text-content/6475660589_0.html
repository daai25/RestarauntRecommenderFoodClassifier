      • Italianità per tutti

        Pizza, pasta, traditional fish and meat dishes, dolci, light seasonal variations: Italy has been at home in our 21 Pizzeria Ristorante Molino for 35 years. Uncomplicated, modern and yet authentically interpreted, Molino relies on in-house recipes and the highest quality. And on the service where Italian charm and expertise score points.

      • Passion for quality

        All Molino dishes served in the modern and pleasant ambience of our pizzeria are freshly prepared. Anything not directly «fatto in casa» has come from our carefully selected producers. For that pinch more Italianità on your plate – buon appetito!
