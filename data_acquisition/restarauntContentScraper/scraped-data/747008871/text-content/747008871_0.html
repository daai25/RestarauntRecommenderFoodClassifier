Welcome

Upon crossing the threshold of the Palais Oriental, you will be swept away on a fabulous journey of the senses to the heart of A Thousand and One Nights. Located in a naturally majestic setting on the shores of Lake Geneva, this grand Moorish-style building invites you to discover the culinary specialities of Iran, Lebanon and Morocco right in the centre of Montreux!

In warm and intimate surroundings enhanced by delicate and refined decoration, immerse yourself in a universe imbued with a subtle mixture of exotic scents and sweetness. Come let yourself be surprised by the fantastic flavours of an authentic cuisine.

Our Restaurant

Delicate and intimate, our restaurant honours Middle Eastern gastronomy in the excellent Arabic-Middle Eastern culinary tradition.

Succumb to the charms of an authentic cuisine characterised by respect for the taste of each ingredient. Enjoy great classics such as chicken couscous, lamb tagine with prunes or shrimp curry and let yourself be tempted by less well-known dishes like minced beef with aubergine, tomatoes, lime and four spices or grilled cubes of lamb fillet.

Banquet Room

Delightfully intimate and private, our magnificent banquet room lends itself to relaxation and conviviality in a sumptuous and refined decor in the best Moorish tradition.

Allow us to serve you for your family gatherings, weddings or business dinners and enjoy a marvellous journey to the heart of A Thousand and One Nights. Nestled in a setting that gracefully and delicately combines architectural art and an exotic gastronomic experience, our banquet room will surprise your guests while providing a warm Middle Eastern atmosphere in a pleasantly intimate environment.

Le Caviar du Palais

Mr. Shahriar Gharibi, the owner of the Oriental Palace started to know caviar already as a child, having been born not far from the Caspian Sea, his close and distant family worked for the producer Shilat.

In Paris, he became familiar with the different aspects of this product. As a manager at the Maison de l'Iran, Avenue des Champs-Elysées, he promoted its marketing.

In Switzerland for more than 30 years, he has been selling caviar to prestigious hotels and restaurants all over Switzerland, to private aviation companies as well as to numerous private customers. He is also an official importer approved by the Swiss authorities.

On request, he organises tastings for caviar lovers and chefs in his shop and laboratory located at rue du quai 8 in Montreux.
