Dear guests and friends

Welcome to the FONDUE HOUSE “DU PONT”!

Enjoyment by the river – enjoyment with a view!
With us you deserve only the very best.

Organic alpine CHEESE FONDUES and RACLETTE,
FONDUE BOURGUIGNONNE (oil) & FONDUE CHINOISE (broth) and chocolate fondue.

We look forward to welcoming you – with tried and tested PREMIUM quality!

Sincerely
Maria, Catia and team

This is the second FONDUE HOUSE in the old city of Luzern/Lucerne, named “DU PONT”, open since February 2018!
