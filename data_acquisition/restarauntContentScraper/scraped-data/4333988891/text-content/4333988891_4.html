activities

Schwändi is a good starting point for trekking and mountain biking.We are less than five kilometres from Glarus and the train station.Skiing and snowboarding during the winter season.

Shopping

The town of Glarus has plenty of little shops and restaurant and is only five minutes away.A number of shopping mall in the region.Driving one hour you reach Zurich or on the other side the designer outlet of Landquart.
For the secondhand market and antiques lovers two shops are nearby.

Art

There are Museum and galleries in the Glarus area.For opening hours and locations check the links page.

Concerts

Many concerts especially during the summer season.Most famous is the Sounds of Glarus.

Ski & Snowboard

The two ski area of Elm and Braunwald are reachable in twenty minutes by car.Flumserberg with over 65 km of ski slopes is 40 minutes away.

Mountain bike

Plenty of track start from Schwändi.At our guests disposal are six full suspension mountain bike.

Swimming

In town is a small public swimming-pool.Five minutes down the road there is a bigger swimming-pool with few small water slide.In less than 30 minutes are two nice lakes.In 40 minutes you reach the Acquapark Alpamare in Pfäffikon.Might be a solution in a raining day.One hour away is the thermal center of Bad ragaz.

Hiking

Schwändi is a good starting point to several trekking.
