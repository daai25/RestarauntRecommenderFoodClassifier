Sahar's Party Service

Celebrate with Sahar – Where every dish tells a story, and every event is a feast!

Make your celebrations unforgettable with Sahar’s Party Services! Whether it’s a birthday, anniversary, corporate event, or any special occasion, we bring the authentic flavors of our cuisine straight to your party. From expertly crafted appetizers to indulgent mains and delightful desserts, our menu is tailored to suit every palate.

Why Choose Sahar Restaurant for Your Event?

  1. Spacious Venue:

    • Enjoy our 150 square meter service area, designed to accommodate events of all sizes.
    • With seating for more than 120 guests and over 20 stand-up spaces, we ensure your guests are comfortable and well-accommodated.

  2. 5-Star Buffet Experience:

    • Treat your guests to a lavish 5-star buffet, featuring a variety of authentic Indian and Pakistani dishes, carefully crafted to please every palate.

  3. State-of-the-Art Amenities:

    • Multimedia and Sound System: Enhance your event with our advanced sound system and projector, perfect for presentations, slideshows, or entertainment.
    • Stage: Create memorable moments on our dedicated stage, ideal for speeches, performances, or special announcements.

  4. Convenient and Accessible:

    • Paid parking is available nearby for the convenience of your guests.
    • Child-friendly environment and wheelchair access ensure all your attendees feel welcome and comfortable.

  5. Seasonal Outdoor Options:

    • Take advantage of our outdoor seating area during the summer for a refreshing and relaxed vibe.
    • Enjoy the large open-air space in front of the restaurant, perfect for gathering, socializing, and capturing beautiful photo moments.

  6. Versatility for Any Occasion:

    • From intimate family gatherings to grand corporate events, we adapt our services to meet your needs.

Additional Perks

  • Our friendly and professional staff will work with you to customize every detail, ensuring a seamless and stress-free event.
  • Located in the heart of Zurich, Sahar Restaurant combines convenience with style, making it the ideal choice for your next celebration.

Celebrate with Us

Let Sahar Restaurant turn your special occasion into an unforgettable experience. With a blend of stunning facilities, exceptional food, and outstanding service, we guarantee a celebration that will leave a lasting impression on you and your guests.
