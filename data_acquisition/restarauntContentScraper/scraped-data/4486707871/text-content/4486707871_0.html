Rasthysi

A small and cosy mountain hut on the hiking and sledging trail nr. 56 between Bort and Bussalp.

Rasthysi is a cozy and charming mountain hut. Here, you can enjoy small meals, refreshing drinks, and a breathtaking view of the Eiger. In the summer, the bus stop is right in front of the house. In the winter, Rasthysi can be reached not only via various hiking trails, but also by sled from First via Waldspitz, or as a small detour from Bussalp.
