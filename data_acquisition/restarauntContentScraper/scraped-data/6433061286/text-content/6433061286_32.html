Shepherd's Room Lenzerheide - Traditional Swiss Mountain Hotel Accommodation

Dreaming in a cocoon

Getting good sleep is sacred to you? We feel the same way. That’s why we looked to nature for ideas and built a cocoon bed that allows you to feel completely protected as you sleep and start the next day refreshed and rejuvenated. Each of the shepherd’s rooms is individually furnished and entirely unique – from cocoon bed to bathtub or shower to gable or ground floor. Here, you will find the home you like the best on 18 to 22 square metres.
