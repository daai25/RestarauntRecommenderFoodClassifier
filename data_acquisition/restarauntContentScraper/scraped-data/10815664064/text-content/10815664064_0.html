The Great Story Of Our Culinary Journey

Kumo means cloud in Japanese, name of our chef.

Izakaya is very famous and popular in Japan like the Tapas bars in Spain and Bistrots in France. It is a friendly place where you can enjoy a beer or a sake accompanied by various snacks and meals.

At Kumo Izakaya you can eat in our cozy dining room, tatami room or take away to simply enjoy our food at home.
