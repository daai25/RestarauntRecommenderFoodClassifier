Lichthof Zentrum

Lunch Menu Garden & Butcher

Due to the floor renovation, the untere and obere canteens will be temporarily closed from July 1, 2025.

As a temporary solution, we will be offering the Garden and Butcher menu lines in the atrium from June 30, 2025 - each from 11.00 am to 2.30 pm. The Lichthof will be open daily from 10.00 am to 4.00 pm. Dinner will not be served during this time.

From August 11, 2025, the lower canteen will be open again as usual (10.00 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.).

We wish you a good time and en Guete!
Your ZFV team

The Lichthof at the University of Zurich's Campus Zentrum is a historic location that is ideal for a quick lunch or a leisurely coffee break. With a wide range of snacks and a selection that particularly appeals to French fries lovers, the Lichthof offers a quick and tasty alternative to the Untere and Obere Mensas.

Events

Whether it's a small, fine coffee break, a creative stand-up lunch or an original aperitif: we are on hand to help you plan and organize your special event. Discover our wide range of services for a successful event and contact us now or order directly online via our Catering E-Shop.
