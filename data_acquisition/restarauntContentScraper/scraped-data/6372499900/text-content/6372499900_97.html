    Great 25mtr indoor pool for swimming lengths
    Great spotlessly clean facility with 25mtr indoor lap pool & separate family pool).

    Only 15mins walk from the old city. Water was a good temp for lengths. Cost €5 for an adult (2019), & this includes an entry card you use to secure a locker (tie key strap round ankle/wrist).

    Changing rooms are shared by men/women but have private cubicles, & toilets are separate. You pass through a shower room before reaching the pool (shared by men & women). There are only 3 private shower cubicles in that room ( I understand you have to pay extra for other showers but I'm not sure where located).

    All facilities were very clean & well maintained. Make sure you take flip-flops as need to take you shoes off b4 entering the changing rooms as soon as you arrive.

    No lane markers for slow/medium/fast, but I was there on a Sunday so not sure if just because of that. Swim anticlockwise (up the right side).
    Review of: Aqualia

    Great place to spend an afternoon indoors!
    We had a rainy, chilly day in Colmar, and which led us to swap some sight-seeing time for someplace warm & dry, and I'm so glad we found this place! They have over 400 games of all different kinds, and you can sit down and play one (or a few) for just a couple of euros per person. We knew the staff could help you find the right game for you & even teach you how to play it, but we were surprised they could do this in both French and English. This was huge, because I only speak a few words in French.

    You can buy snacks and drinks (with or without alcohol) to enjoy while you pay, and you can even buy the games to keep. We ordered a few hard drinks, and they were delicious. As an added bonus, the fee to play the games goes down with every round of drinks you buy.

    Overall, we enjoyed the time we spent here, and I highly recommend this place. I wish we had one in my home town!
    Review of: L'Aliane Bar Boutiquo-Jeux
