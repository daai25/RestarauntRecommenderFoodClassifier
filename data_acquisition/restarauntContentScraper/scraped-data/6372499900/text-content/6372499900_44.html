About
Antarctic Logistics & Expeditions (ALE) is the polar leader in Antarctic adventures for travelers and explorers of all activity levels. ALE has provided Antarctic guided experiences, air transportation, logistic support for 30 years to the interior of Antarctica. With 15 experiences and 26 departure dates we have a desire to share the white continent with other passionate explorers.

Experience of a lifetime delivered flawlessly
Jan 2023 • Friends
We went for a skydive in Antarctica.

Every single staff member was an exceptional human being. Highly knowledgeable and experienced team. They went above and beyond to make us feel comfortable. Comfort level was 10/10 in such a harsh environment, we did not expect that it was possible.

Brittni, Avi & Brook made us feel right at home immediately. Their ice sculpturing work was spectacular and individualised for us. I mean the work - just WOW.

Lectures that were organised were truly unforgettable. Katja, Collin, Avi & Christopher were very well read & researched with fascinating facts and stories.Never a dull moment with them.

Excursions were enjoyable. Guides were perfect. Especially Christian, he was amazing, knowledgeable, caring. We learned a lot from him.

Our safety was never in question, they legit thought about everything and anticipated things very well. Josh & team made extra sure and were constantly vigilant.

Their gift shop was full of high quality one of a kind merchandise.

The food was exquisite. Very considerate to food preferences/ vegan/halal/vegetarian/ gluten free.
Every meal was perfect.

The staff at the Punta arenas office was so experienced, everything was super smooth.
Big shout out to Alberto and Ranata who were our team managers. They had extra high quality for rental in every size imaginable.

Transfers and transport was very smooth.

The rest of the staff, which we met briefly and the ones who were behind the scenes- also Mega Thank you. Your hard work did not go unnoticed.

Big Mike was so humble and warm to us. Man what an adventure we had with ALE. I thought Antarctica was a once in a lifetime place to go to. But they activities they offer and the level of service just compels you to plan another trip.
