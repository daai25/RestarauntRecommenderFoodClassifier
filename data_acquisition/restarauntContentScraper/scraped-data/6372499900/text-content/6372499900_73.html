Neighborhood: Hradcany & Letna
Your experience in Prague will never be complete if you do not see it from the top. Hillsides, stretched from the west to the north, provide spectacular views of greenish copper cupolas, narrow cobblestone streets, bridges connecting both river banks and green gardens from red-roof tops of centuries-old houses. Prague pulls you into its 1300 year old history. Walking up the hill or stairs to Prague Castle, the historical seat of Czech rulers and the majestic Saint Vitus Cathedral can leave you speechless (and out of breath). Luckily, plenty of terrace cafés overlooking gardens and beautiful parks are available to provide rest and a place to catch your breath again.

Elaborately Decorated Library Rooms Where the Monastery Served as Keeper of Knowledge
Nov 2023 • Couples
Monasteries like Strahov were not just places of worship but keepers of knowledge. The Strahov Library inside the Strahov Monastery has a library of philosophy and a library of theology. Both rooms contain hundreds of books dating as far back as the 10th century and the elaborately decorated rooms with ornate ceilings are stunning to behold. Just the sight of them is worth the price of admission. However, there is also so much history in here. As keepers of knowledge, the monastery could also decide what information should be shared and which should be suppressed. In the Library of Theology, there is a golden locked case containing the "prohibited books" from such "controversial" figures such as Copernicus, Jan Hus and Rousseau. In the room connecting the two libraries, there are interesting displays of other articles of science that can be seen. It costs 150 CZK to enter the monastery, and it is well worth it. The monastery often closes for an hour each afternoon so confirm opening times before visiting.
