    Hong Kong, China187,227 contributions
    Bartholdi's fountain monument dedicated to Lazare de Schwendi
    This is a statue fountain monument at Place de l'Ancienne Douane that dedicated to Lazare de Schwendi (1522-83), military general to Emperor Maximilian II and Lord of Hohlandsburg. It is the work of Auguste Bartholdi and was erected in 1898.

    What you see is a bronze of Schwendi in armour, raising his his right hand while holding the vines of Tokay, a variety of grape from the region. The statue stands upon an ornamental pedestal with extended spouts which spill water into a ground level basin below. The fountain was not in operation during our recent December visit, instead filled with Christmas wreath, garlands and other holiday decorations.
