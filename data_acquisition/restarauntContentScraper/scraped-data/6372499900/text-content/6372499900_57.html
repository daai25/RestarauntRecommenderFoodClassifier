About
Daily departures to Blue Cave at 9.00 am, 12 noon and 3.00 pm. The tour lasts for 3 hours. The boat trip includes panoramic sightseeing of Kotor Bay, Tivat Bay and Herceg Novi Bay. Departure from Port of Kotor. It takes 50 minutes to get to the Blue Cave. There we have a 30 minute break (for a swim if you wish). After that we continue to Mamula Island, where we have a 10 to 15 minute break for a panoramic view. We then move on to the submarine Tunnel, and inside of it we have a 10 minute photo stop. Our next stop is at Our Lady of the Rock Island, where you can spend 20 minutes visiting the church and the museum. After that we continue for a panoramic view of Perast for 10 minutes, after which we return to Kotor.
