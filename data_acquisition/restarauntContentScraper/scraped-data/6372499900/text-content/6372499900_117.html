    Great place to spend an afternoon indoors!
    We had a rainy, chilly day in Colmar, and which led us to swap some sight-seeing time for someplace warm & dry, and I'm so glad we found this place! They have over 400 games of all different kinds, and you can sit down and play one (or a few) for just a couple of euros per person. We knew the staff could help you find the right game for you & even teach you how to play it, but we were surprised they could do this in both French and English. This was huge, because I only speak a few words in French.

    You can buy snacks and drinks (with or without alcohol) to enjoy while you pay, and you can even buy the games to keep. We ordered a few hard drinks, and they were delicious. As an added bonus, the fee to play the games goes down with every round of drinks you buy.

    Overall, we enjoyed the time we spent here, and I highly recommend this place. I wish we had one in my home town!
