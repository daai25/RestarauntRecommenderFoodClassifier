About
Claimed to be the World's Largest Covered Butterfly Garden. The circular garden has ten custom built domes and a butterfly museum. The ten domes are divided into four (4) sections. Each dome is filled with thousands lovely butterflies, 50 varieties of butterfly in different sizes and colors naturally alive, flying around the visitors, and standing over the flowers and the plants. Also, many nectar plants with different colors are planted to feed the butterflies. The domes are climate-controlled, providing the butterflies a relaxed environment and permitting the domes to be open throughout the year.

The area

Neighborhood: Al Barsha
Al Barsha's allure lies in its seamless blend of contemporary and traditional Dubai. This neighborhood is home to the Mall of the Emirates, a shopping haven teeming with high-end boutiques and eateries, and also boasts the indoor ski resort, Ski Dubai, providing a delightful contrast of desert heat and alpine chill. Cultural enthusiasts will appreciate the Middle Eastern Art collection at the Meem Gallery, while food lovers can indulge in authentic Emirati cuisine at Al Fanar Restaurant. With the Dubai Metro's red line running through Al Barsha, accessing other parts of the city is a breeze. Al Barsha's charm is in its ability to offer a tranquil residential atmosphere amidst the city's dynamic pulse.
