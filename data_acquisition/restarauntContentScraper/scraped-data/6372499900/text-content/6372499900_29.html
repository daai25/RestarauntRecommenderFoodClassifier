    About Yeti Airlines

    Airline Summary

    Yeti Airlines (YT) is an airline based in Kathmandu. Together with its subsidiary Tara Air, it is the largest domestic carrier in Nepal. The airline operates from a hub at Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM). Biratnagar Airport (BIR), Gautam Buddha Airport (BWA) and Pokhara Airport (PKR) have been designated as focus cities. Yeti Airlines flies to 10 domestic destinations, as well as an hour-long Everest Express mountain flight. The airline's fleet consists of seven BAe Jetstream 41, each of which is configured with a single Economy Class cabin.
