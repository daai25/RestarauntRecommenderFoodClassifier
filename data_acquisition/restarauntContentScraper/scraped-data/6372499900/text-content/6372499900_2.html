Accessibility Statement

At Tripadvisor, we strive to ensure our platform empowers travel for all. We are committed to making our website and app accessible, and work in constant pursuit of designing and building inclusive products. As of June 2025, we have carefully examined and made improvements to the purchase flow of our bookable travel experiences, or Things To Do. Our platform now largely addresses the latest requirements for e-commerce services as set by the European Accessibility Act (EAA). The EAA requires that websites serving European consumers meet or exceed the WCAG 2.1 AA standard when they offer certain specified types of services. Alongside other tech leaders championing digital accessibility, Tripadvisor will continue working to provide you with the best experience possible. You can email us at accessibility@tripadvisor.com with suggestions for further improvements tied to the accessibility of our website and app.
