About
Learn more about New Zealand's indigenous Maori people during this evening of entertainment. You'll be welcomed to the Te Puia cultural center by a traditional powhiri ceremony, followed by a cultural show. Enjoy a dinner of many Kiwi favorites, including the traditional Maori hangi, cooked in an underground pit. End the evening with a tour of the geothermal valley of bubbling mud pools and geysers.

      A Cultural Event With Hangi Dinner Was Outstanding
      Mar 2020
      For those that have attended a Hawaiian luau I had expectations this would be the same. As it turns out I was wrong. Sure, the pig comes out of the earth. The dinner is served buffet style. But that doesn't do this event justice.

      Let me back track. This organization picked us up at our hotel. Upon being delivered to the entrance the cultural portion of the evening started with a greeting to all the guests, perhaps 100 people. A history of this site and the Maori people was then recited.

      We were then lead through the grounds of this large property to look at some of the ancient artifacts including a really long war canoe. At the end of the tour we watched the pig be raised from the oven pit, or "umu."We then created a semi-circle in front of a communal house, or "wharenui". At this point the the traditional Maori dance, the "haka" commenced.

      One of the guests was presented to the Maori tribesman protecting the wharenui. Once it was established we had come in peace, we all filed into the wharenui for more haka ceremony. A couple of the men were invited onto a stage to learn some of the moves. It was all very entertaining.

      We then moved onto the actual hangi dinner. We were directed to long tables by the number of people in our group. The seating is in a beautiful large dining room with plenty of space. They invite tables to the buffet lines one at a time. There is plenty of variety for even those most persnickety eater.

      At the end of the evening you are invited to go down to the thermal pools that exist on the property. Our group declined to do this portion of the event and instead headed for the buses that returned us to the hotel. But by no means did we feel like we missed out since it was a full evening.
