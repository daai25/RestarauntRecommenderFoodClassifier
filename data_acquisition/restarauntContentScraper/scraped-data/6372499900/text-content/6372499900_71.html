Awesome fun for kids
Jun 2023 • Family
It’s easy to find. We rented a Jeep for this excursion! It was so worth the $30. The animals spill the food all in the car! You’ll be cleaning out pellets for months if you don’t. Each person gets like a quart container of feed for free. But you can buy a bucket for about $25. The General Store is amazing. You can find anything from groceries to baked good to gift there. I plan to go back and do some Christmas shopping for Mississippi made gifts.

The actual ride thru the zoo is great. The animals come straight up to the car. We saw anything from ostriches, emu’s, Buffalo, deer, horses, even saw a momma pig and her tiny pig. The whole ride took about 25 minutes but the Jeep rental was for an hour so we made our way around again.

We took 2 of our oldest grandchildren (ages 14 and 9) and will definitely bring our youngest (age 3) when they come to town.

You will love the experience.

Great safe Safari adventure for kids of all ages.
Aug 2020 • Family
What a great Covid-safe fun adventure for my grandkids 13, 9 and 5. Masks and distancing were enforced when inside a building. Pay the safari entrance fee and drive the animal loop as many times as you want. Go EARLY! The animals are less hungry as others travel the loop and fed them. You can buy extra food too.

Lunch was great. Buffet style BUT served by staff, wearing gloves and a mask. Return to the buffet all you want but no self service.

The petting zoo is free but carrots are for sale to feed those animals if you want. There is a mom and baby giraffe in the petting zoo, but be warned...if you want to feed the giraffe, it’s an extra $5.00 person.

It was great for something to do in these difficult times.
