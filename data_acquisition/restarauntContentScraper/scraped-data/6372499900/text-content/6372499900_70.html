The area

Address
Neighborhood: Centro Storico
The pulsating core of Milan is in Centro. Duomo Square is enclosed by the gothic cathedral, the historic icon of Milan. However, shopping is what draws most crowds to this area. The classy and versatile Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II with luxury boutiques and high quality restaurants will turn any shopping or lunch expedition into a royal experience. Head to the rooftop of the flagship La Rinascente to enjoy the view of the animated square with a panorama of the Duomo. The Corso Vittorio Emanuele II is the street for high-end shopping. Brands of all kinds and street artists attract the attention of all visitors; while at Via Santa Radegonda 16, Luini Panzerotti is a much appreciated street food shop providing unimaginable culinary experiences. If shopping takes its toll on you, consider visiting Castello Sforzesco, a 15th century castle housing several museums. Those looking to relax can go to Parco Sempione, where nature provides respite from the energy of this dynamic and international city.

For Milan it is an institution
May 2025 • Couples
The Rinascente Duomo is truly an institution in Milan!
Here it was born in 1865 as "Magazzini Bocconi" to which, rebuilt after a fire, D'Annunzio gave, in 1917, the new auspicious name of "la Rinascente". The assortment is at the top, both for brand and variety.
Today it is spread over a basement, a ground floor, a mezzanine, seven floors of sale (of which the seventh entirely dedicated to food and catering - reviewed separately) in the main building, plus an Annex with three levels of sale, connected to the main building.
It is preferable to go there in the morning: there are always lots of people, but maybe a little less.
