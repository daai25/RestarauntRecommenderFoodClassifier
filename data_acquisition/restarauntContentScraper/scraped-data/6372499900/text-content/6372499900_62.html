About
A west coast–themed dinner buffet and live musical entertainment accompany the views on this leisurely sunset cruise. Don’t miss Vancouver’s waterfront sights while you dine; the boat takes you from the shimmering skyline to the West Vancouver shoreline. It’s the best spot in Vancouver to watch the sun slowly disappear into the Pacific Ocean.

    The tour operator has provided the following answers to customers’ questions.
    Is it reserved seating?
    Yes, all seats are reserved based on your booking (similar to a restaurant). Please let us know at the time of booking, or no later than 72 hours prior to the cruise if you wish to be seated with other travelers who have booked separately.
    Answered Nov 2022
    Is it wheelchair accessible?
    Our vessels are wheelchair possible. If you have a standard-sized wheelchair, it would be no problem to get you on the vessel. However, there are stairs to the upper decks. This means you will be seated on the main deck that you boarded on. There are windows all around the vessel for panoramic viewing of the surroundings. Furthermore, the accessibility of the washrooms varies between each vessel. If you are in a wheelchair please let us know at the time of booking.
    Answered Nov 2022
    When do I need to arrive to the docks?
    Boarding for our dining cruises is 30 minutes prior to the start time. We leave on time, please do not be late.
    Answered Nov 2022
    Do I need a boarding pass?
    Yes you do. Please check in at the tour and information office prior to boarding the vessel to collect your physical boarding pass. The tour office and vessels are at the same location at 501.Denman Street.
    Answered Nov 2022
