At Cheval Blanc by Peter Knogl, French haute cuisine meets Mediterranean and Asian influences. With finesse, precision, and passion, Chef de Cuisine Peter Knogl creates culinary masterpieces – pure and authentic, inspired by nature’s finest ingredients. The result? Flavour compositions that not only surprise but touch all the senses. No wonder Cheval Blanc ranks among the world’s 100 best restaurants – awarded 3 Michelin stars and 19 GaultMillau points.

Look forward to creations that captivate. Flavours that linger in memory. A culinary experience that resonates.
