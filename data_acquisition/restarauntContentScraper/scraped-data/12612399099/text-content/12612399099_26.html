Meetings at Widder Hotel

There is so much to celebrate: anniversaries, business achievements, a milestone on life’s highway or simply life itself. And Widder Hotel is the ideal venue for any occasion. What’s more, our hotel’s infrastructure and prime location at the centre of Zurich make it suitable for business meetings too. Our team looks forward to hosting your event with attentive, bespoke service.

Luxury fed by nature

The Living Circle – is a hand-picked group of first-class hotels and restaurants in incomparably beautiful locations, run by dedicated hosts. Rice, vegetables, fruit and a whole array of exquisite products – including wine, of course – is whisked fresh from our own farms directly to your table. That is how we define luxury. That is The Living Circle – luxury fed by nature.
