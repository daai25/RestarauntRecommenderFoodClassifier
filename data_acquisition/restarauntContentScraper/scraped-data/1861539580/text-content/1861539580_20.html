Our story

Restaurant Indian
Tamarind Garden- Zurich

We started our dream restaurant in Zurich in 2019 with the following goals in mind:

  • Providing a pleasant authentic North Indian culinary experience in the Zürich region.
  • Through our experience and dedication, we have become masters of the trade.
  • We provide authentic North Indian Food that is fresh, healthy and simply unforgettable.

Menu

We serve the best and highest quality Indian food, including HALAL meat, vegan options, chicken, and fish.

  • Aloo Tikki CHF 9.50

    A favorite dish from the city of Patiala in northwestern Punjab; pan fried potato cakes accompanied by a tamarind-date sauce, mint and green apple chutney.

  • Onion & Potato Fritters CHF 9.50

    Flavoured with mild spices served with mint sauce and mango chutney.

  • Lamb Seekh Kabab CHF 12.50

    Hand stuffed lamb mixed with spices, cooked on skewers in the tandoor and served with mint sauce.

  • Keema Naan CHF 13.50

    Fresh naan, topped with minced lamb, cooked with ginger, garlic and garam masala.

  • Chicken Samosa CHF 9.50

    Crispy fried pastry parcels stuffed with potato and peas or with minced chicken with hand pounded spices.

  • Hara Bhara Kabab CHF 12.50

    Vegetable kebab cooked in the tandoor, served with apple and mint chutney.

  • Bhutani Prawns CHF 16.50

    Pan fried king prawns with garlic, butter, mustard seeds and curry leaves.

  • Hydrabadi Tikka CHF 13.50

    Breast of chicken marinated with yoghurt, fresh mint and chilli served with beetroot and mango-chutney.
