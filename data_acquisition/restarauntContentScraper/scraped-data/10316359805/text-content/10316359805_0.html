Our narrative

I am kirtimalini Gadre, originally from Kalyan (Maharashtra, India) resides here in switzerland since 2006 .I worked as software professional till 2018 with different banks and companies. Along with IT work I kept serving Indian society with my cooking capacity and skill in almost all social and charity events! I am foody and like to analyze and cook recipes in the best way according to the benchmark taste I have acquired in India. This characteristic and passion of cooking led to the idea of serving Indian Food in a more professional way . My aim is to provide good quality tasty and price worth food to Swiss locals and Indians residing in Switzerland with attitude of Serving (Sewa) Krushnarpanmastu!
