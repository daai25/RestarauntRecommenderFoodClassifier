Ristorante Azzurro - Terra e Mare in Bern

Experience true Italian hospitality with us in the city of Bern. The « Azzurro - Terra e Mare », awarded the exclusive « Ospitalita Italiana » seal, is one of the best Italian restaurants. Impressive dishes, selected wines and the unique Mediterranean ambience guarantee a pleasurable experience.

The Ristorante Azzurro offers about 120 - 130 guests a cosy atmosphere in the Terra room or in the special Mare room, where a large seawater aquarium is the centre of attention, which will delight young and old alike. When the weather is fine, enjoy the sun on the covered terrace of the restaurant, just like in Italy.
