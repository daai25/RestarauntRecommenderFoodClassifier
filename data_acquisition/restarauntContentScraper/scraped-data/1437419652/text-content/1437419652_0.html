Scopri il menù

The idea is based on essentiality and takes the form of a simple kitchen that enhances the raw materials used.


A small room heated by the fire of a fireplace creates a welcoming and intimate environment for our guests; summer terrace enhances the cooler air Centovalli on hot summer days.


The menu is essential. However, the choice of dishes varies, creating a dynamism and an alternation of the proposals, especially based on the seasonality of the raw materials;

definitely try the autumn proposals based on game.

You can also find a series of products ready to be enjoyed at home.
