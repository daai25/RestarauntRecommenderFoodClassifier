Le Sommet

A summit getaway means having Mont Blanc, the Dents du Midi and Lake Geneva as table neighbours.

  • At 2002 m altitude, accessible by funicular and cable car or on foot
  • 135 seats inside / 240 seats on the terrace
  • Food and drinks available for self-service
  • Seminar room for 10 to 35 people
