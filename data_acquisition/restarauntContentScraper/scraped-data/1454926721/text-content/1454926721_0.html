About us

The Café-Restaurant Le Soleil in Bretigny-sur-Morrens has always been able to identify very early the needs of its customers and innovates constantly in order to give you an unforgettable pleasure.

Offering Indian and European cuisine, the restaurant is managed by motivated and qualified people. Today the company is fully part of the local life of Bretigny-sur-Morrens and radiates in the whole region of Gros-de-Vaud.
