The Dining Area

The Beer Garden

Tadka’s 100 seat room, “The Beer Garden” features a temperature-controlled wine cellar, displays of more wine and a private atmosphere. The exquisite greenery perfectly blends with the fresh drinks and chilled beer served here making your dining experience worthwhile.

The dining Crown

Overlooking the Beer Garden the Dining Crown, can seat up to 30 guests in the dining area. The Dining Crown is a modern, bright and elegant private dining space nestled in the center of the restaurant.
