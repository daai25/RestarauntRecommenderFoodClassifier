Meet the team

Our talents and experience are diverse, but at TADKA, we’re united by one thing: an overarching passion for Indian food, wine, and hospitality.

Every day we make many decisions about what we eat and every one of our food choices has an ethical and environmental impact. It is the best of coincidences that good food choices – those that are good for our land and people, for the environment, for the body and mind – are also delicious and enjoyable.
