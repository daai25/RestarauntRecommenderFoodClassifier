Welcome in Bel-Air

Welcome to our hotel, restaurant and cellar located in the heart of Praz in Vully Fribourgeois. Our establishment is the result of a long family tradition dating back to 1900!


Ideally located on the shores of Lake Murten, we are located about twenty minutes from large neighboring cities such as Fribourg, Bern, Neuchâtel. The charm of the lakeside, the tranquility of the countryside make this place an ideal place to spend your holidays or to rest a few days.


Whether you are at Bel-Air to eat, relax for a few days or to work, we will do our best to exceed your expectations.
