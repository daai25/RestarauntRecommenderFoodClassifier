​About Us

​

Welcome to Namaste India Bern, an authentic North Indian restaurant located in the heart of Bern. We take pride in bringing the rich flavors of India to Switzerland, offering a diverse selection of traditional dishes prepared with fresh ingredients and aromatic spices.

At Namaste India Bern, we are passionate about providing a warm and inviting dining experience, where guests can enjoy classic North Indian cuisine, from flavorful curries to sizzling tandoori specialties. Whether you're looking for a casual meal, a family gathering, or a special celebration, our restaurant is the perfect destination for an unforgettable culinary journey.

Experience the true taste of India, right here in Bern!
