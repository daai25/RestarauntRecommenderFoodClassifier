Namaste India – Neuengass-Passage, Bern

 

Welcome to Namaste India Neuengass-Passage, your destination for authentic North Indian cuisine in the heart of Bern. Conveniently located at Neuengass-Passage 3, 3011 Bern, we offer a true taste of India with traditional recipes, rich flavors, and high-quality ingredients.

At Namaste India Neuengass-Passage, we take pride in serving a variety of Indian specialties, from flavorful curries and sizzling tandoori dishes to aromatic biryanis and delicious vegetarian and vegan options. Whether you're looking for a quick lunch, a cozy dinner, or takeaway and delivery services, we have something for everyone.
