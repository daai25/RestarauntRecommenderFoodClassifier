Right in the midst of the action

Enjoy our specialties till late in the evening and enjoy the wide entertainment offers around the neighbourhood.

In one of Zurich’s most lively, trendy and diverse neighbourhoods, our restaurant has been a fixture since 1996. The classical interior takes you back in time and displays beautiful Chinese craftworks. The blend of the warm red colours with the stones and woods gives the restaurant a cosy atmosphere.

In the heart of Zurich

The restaurant is just a few minutes from Zurich Main Station and is centrally located in one of Zurich’s most popular neighbourhoods.

Long opening hours

Langstrasse does not sleep. Comes nightfall, the districts nightlife keeps everyone entertained. Our restaurant is in the midst of the action and has long opening hours to satisfy your Asian food cravings.

Fantastic food options

Our menu card offers a wide selection of dishes that will satisfy all your cravings. From crispy duck, steaming dumpling to wholesome noodle soups, we have you covered.

A neighbourhood fixture

Suan Long opened in 1996 and has since then won the heart of many locals, expats and tourists and is today a neighbourhood icon.
