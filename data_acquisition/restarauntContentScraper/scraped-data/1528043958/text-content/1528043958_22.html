Who we are ?

Welcome to Garhwal, a place where each dish tells the profound story of a family's love for Indian cuisine. We, the Rawat family, warmly welcome each guest with the warmth that has always been a cornerstone of our heritage. At the forefront of our culinary journey are brothers Rajender Singh and Narendra Singh, chefs with extensive experience in the art of cooking. Chef Rajender Singh, with over 30 years of culinary practice, honors his father, the late Chattar Singh—a celebrated chef across India, Muscat, Germany, and Hong Kong from 1949 to 1994. His expertise, a legacy handed down through generations, resonates in every spice-rich bite. Chef Narendra Singh brings over 20 years of experience, contributing his own legacy of flavors and techniques. Together, they create a powerful culinary duo: Rajender refined his craft for nearly 16 years at the Kerala restaurant in Zürich, and Narendra dedicated roughly 9 years to the same esteemed establishment. The generous Ujla Devi Rawat, supported by her sons Vikash and Akash, completes our family, ensuring every visit is as delightful as the flavors of our dishes. After our days at Kerala, we are here once again in Zürich to welcome you, our esteemed guests. Our menu is a celebration of the Garhwal region's culinary treasures, from the peaks of the Himalayas right onto your plate. For our friends who adore Kerala cuisine, we have kept our kitchen fires burning just for you, to ensure that these beloved flavors continue to be part of our narrative. Garhwal is more than just a restaurant; it is a place where celebrations are transformed into enchanted occasions with custom buffets for birthdays, weddings, and more. We are committed to creating a dining experience as unique as our guests themselves. Whether you are longing for traditional Indian dishes or wish to explore something entirely new, our chefs are ready to create something that will utterly satisfy your senses. Step into the world of Garhwal for a culinary adventure where every meal is a loving gesture from us to you. We eagerly await the opportunity to serve you the very essence of India, prepared with passion and presented with great care. Welcome to our family at Garhwal.
