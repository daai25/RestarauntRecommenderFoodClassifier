UMAMIDO RIVE

GENEVA
Apr 11
Written By Stef Hamerlinck

COURS DE RIVE 18, 1207 GenEVA

 
 

Slurp, savor and smile at Umamido Rive!

Located in the bustling city center of Geneva, Umamido Rive offers some of the most beloved noodles in the area! Featuring an open kitchen where you can see our dishes come to life and a friendly team ready to welcome you, our restaurant is the perfect place for a satisfying lunch or a comforting dinner before enjoying Geneva's nightlife. Stop by Umamido Rive to experience our straightforward and delicious ramen, crafted with care and served in a relaxed and inviting atmosphere. It's an ideal spot to unwind and indulge in genuine flavors!
