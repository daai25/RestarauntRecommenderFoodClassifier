DISCOVER AMMOS RESTAURANT

Welcome to
Ammos Restaurant - Lucerne

 
With us you will experience Authentic tastes of Greek and Mediterranean kitchen. We serve some classics and also surprising Greek Meze. Enjoy it with the fitting music, the rushing river and in an ambience of feeling like you are on vacation.

Vegetarian guests and with celiac disease will be pleasant surprised because the Greek kitchen offers many dishes which you can eat without hesitate.

We look forward to welcome you with a warm “Kali mera” and surprise you with our passion to be your host.
