FLAVOURS MOTIVATION

We congratulate Faisal Khan on the opening of the new Restaurant „Khan‘s - flavours of asia“ @KhanAsia https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100068607518063 Horgen. The ‘impressive set menus guarantee a unique and luxurious dining experience, each offers a culinary journey through India. Be it the chats from North, dosas from South or Momos from East, all available on the Menu. But of course you can also order à la carte (you will find a large selection for vegetarians too!). All dishes are cooked with love and great passion for the culinary traditions of India, served by Khan’s attentive and sympathic staff. If you are looking for a great place for lunch: Khan‘s offers a very affordable lunch buffet from monday to friday.
