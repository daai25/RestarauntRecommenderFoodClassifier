Al Battello Hotel & Restaurant

Located in the charming village of Morcote, one of the jewels of Lake Lugano, Al Battello is more than just a restaurant with lodging. Here, hospitality blends with tradition, offering our guests a unique and unforgettable experience. For thirty-three years, Milan and Radica have run this corner of paradise with passion and attention to detail, ensuring a warm and discreet welcome.
/en/booking/room

Imagine yourself strolling under the ancient arcades that overlook the shores of the Ceresio, breathing in the tranquility and beauty of the lakeside landscape. Whether you are looking for a place to relax, a romantic retreat or a culinary adventure, Al Battello is the ideal destination for you.

The Restaurant

Al Battello Restaurant is a temple of taste, where local culinary tradition meets creativity and passion. Our dishes are prepared with fresh and genuine ingredients, following recipes handed down through generations. From lake fish specialties to the authentic flavors of Ticino cuisine, each course is a sensory journey that tells the story and culture of the area.
