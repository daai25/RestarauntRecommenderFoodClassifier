Swiss cuisine with tradition

At Wirtshaus Taube, Lucerne and Swiss dishes are freshly prepared according to old original recipes. Just like in grandmother's day. The traditional tavern is located in the small town of Lucerne and welcomes you in a rustic ambience. In summer, you can also enjoy our authentic dishes on the terrace by the world-famous Nadelwehr directly on the Reuss.

We look forward to your visit!
