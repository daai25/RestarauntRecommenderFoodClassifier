Welcome to the Hotel Restaurant Bahnhof Zollikofen
Your hotel-restaurant-pizzeria right in front of the train station in Zollikofen

Very well located - only two stops from the capital Bern - approx. 7 min. For the BEA-Expo it is only one stop - approx. 4 minutes.

In the in-house restaurant, you can enjoy a large selection of Italian dishes as well as wood-fired oven pizzas and exquisite "good home cooking" dishes. WiFi is available in all areas of the property free of charge. In addition, private parking spaces are available free of charge (while stocks last)

We look forward to your visit and are convinced that we can offer you an all-round enjoyable and inspiring stay
