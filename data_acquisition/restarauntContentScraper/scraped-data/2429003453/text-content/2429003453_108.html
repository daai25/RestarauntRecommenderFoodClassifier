Cross-country skiing centre in Waldweid

In the forest around the Wasserfallen-Passwang recreation area the Reigoldswil ski club runs a cross-country skiing centre with cross-country ski runs with a total length of 7 km. The cross-country ski runs are located at 980 - 1,040 m above sea level. There are challenging runs for ambitious training and simple loops for pleasure skiers or beginners. All of the cross-country ski runs are regularly prepared with a modern detector for classical and free (skating) cross-country skiing techniques.

Information

Cross-country ski run network
The cross-country ski runs start from the Restaurant Waldweide or above the mountain station of the Reigoldswil-Wasserfallen cable car. There are tracks available for free techniques (skating) and traditional cross-country skiing.

Panoramic cross-country ski run (2.5 km): challenging, wonderful views of the Alps and Mittelland
Michael Vogt cross-country ski run (2.0 km): easy, the beginners’ loop

Changing opportunities
There is a heated changing room available in the Restaurant Waldweide free of charge.

Getting here
From Reigoldswil (BL) with the modern cable car in 12 minutes to Wasserfallen. 20 minutes on foot to the designated cross-country ski run.
From Waldenburg (BL) by car to the forest. Parking spaces available at the restaurant. Warning: steep road not completely cleared, winter equipment absolutely essential.

Prices

There is a charge of CHF 6.oo per day for using the cross-country ski runs. Holders of a valid cross-country ski run pass from "Switzerland cross-country ski runs" can use the runs for free. The passes are valid throughout Switzerland and can be purchased as follows:

Price of CHF 140.00, incl. discount / voucher for CHF 20.00 that can be used in Schmutz Sport AG in Oberdorf and Nordic Sport in Frenkendorf from a purchase value of CHF 75.00.

Points of sale: Schmutz Sport AG in Oberdorf, Nordic Sport GmbH in Frenkendorf, Restaurant Waldweide, order (Skiclub Reigoldswil, Remo Suter, Rüschelgasse 7a, 4418 Reigoldswil, 061 941 24 85).
