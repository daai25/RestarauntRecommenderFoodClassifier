Welcome to the Restaurant des Gorges du Durnand

Located at the foot of the magnificent natural site of the Gorges du Durnand, just 5 minutes from Martigny, our restaurant welcomes you in a dynamic and friendly atmosphere. Open from February to December, it’s the ideal stop-off for a gourmet break before or after your visit to the gorges.

Fresh and spectacular

Just like the torrent that rushes between the rock faces, we offer fresh, enthusiastic regional cuisine all year round.

Enjoy a gourmet break

Our chef offers cuisine to suit all budgets, with dishes such as snails, perch fillets, beef fillet and our famous fondue. Come and discover our local flavours and homemade specialities.

Local flavours and home-made specialities

Let yourself be tempted by the local and seasonal flavours prepared by our chef. Don’t miss our signature speciality, the ‘Fondue des Gorges’.

March: Brittany comes to us with a special fish menu, bringing the delights of the ocean straight to your table.

Autumn: Savour our hunting menu for an unforgettable culinary experience. A feast for the palate!

Carnotzet – Banquet Hall

For your group outings or family meals, our banqueting room, which can accommodate 20 to 40 people, offers a warm, intimate atmosphere. Contact us to create a menu tailored to your needs.

Come and enjoy an exceptional culinary experience at Gorges du Durnand, where every visit becomes a real gastronomic adventure.
