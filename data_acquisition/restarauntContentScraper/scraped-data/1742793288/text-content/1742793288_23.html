Contact & access

Access by car

Five minutes from Martigny
Take the Martigny-Croix motorway exit
Follow signs for Gd-St-Bernard at roundabout
After 2 km, turn right and enter Les Valettes
Follow signs for Champex-Lac

Parking

Free parking for visitors to the Gorges du Durnand and for guests of the restaurant and holiday apartment.
For coaches, please call before your arrival (phone +41 27 722 20 77), as our car park has limited space. Thank you for your understanding !

Access by train

Take the Martigny-Orsières train to Bovernier. It’s then a 20 minute walk following the signs for Les Valettes and Champex.
