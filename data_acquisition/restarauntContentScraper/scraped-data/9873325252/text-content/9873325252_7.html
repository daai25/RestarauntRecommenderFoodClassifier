Frequently Asked Questions

  • The Gyros is served as a plate; perfect for those with a hearty appetite. It includes rotisserie slices of your choice of meat: chicken, pork, or vegan, accompanied by homemade Pita Bread, Tzatziki (Greek yogurt dip with cucumbers and garlic), and a side of potatoes, onions, and tomatoes.

    Alternatively, if you're looking for a quick, on-the-go option, our bestseller, the Souvlaki is the way to go. It includes the same mouthwatering ingredients, but in a wrap, for convenience.

  • A Freddo Espresso is a cold double shot of espresso. Similarly, a Freddo Cappuccino is a Freddo Espresso topped with cold foamed milk!

  • Our produce (fruits and vegetables) are sourced locally, from Switzerland. However, our other core ingredients are sourced straight from Greece! Everything from our Gyros meat, to virgin olive oil and olives, to our Greek pastries like Bougatsa and Spanakopita!

  • A Greek salad is composed of cucumbers, red onions, green bell peppers, cherry tomatoes, capers, kalamata olives, and Greek feta topped with Cretan olive oil and greek oregano. Ntakos, on the other hand, has two additional key ingredients such as Ntakos (hard barley rusk), our homemade tomato sauce.
