Destination

Your Retreat in Wengen

Nestled on a sun-kissed terrace in the heart of the Swiss Alps, Hotel Regina offers a unique blend of charm and elegance in the picturesque village of Wengen. Perched 400 meters above the Lauterbrunnen Valley, at an altitude of 1,274 meters, our hotel provides an idyllic mountain retreat with sweeping views of the Jungfrau and beyond.

Wengen, a car-free, alpine gem, is a place where tradition meets modern comfort. Its nostalgic wooden chalets and grand Belle Époque hotels paint a picture of timeless beauty, preserving the authentic character of this mountain village.

Whether you're seeking thrilling outdoor adventures or a peaceful escape surrounded by nature, Wengen's sunny, wind-protected location offers year-round allure. The south-west-facing terrace ensures that you’ll enjoy more sunshine here than most places in the region—perfect for soaking in the breathtaking alpine scenery.

HOW TO REACH US

Accessible only by the Wengernalp Railway since 1893, Wengen remains an oasis of tranquility, free from the hustle of cars and city life. Guests can easily reach us from Lauterbrunnen, where parking facilities are available.

The scenic train ride takes just 15 minutes, offering stunning views of the valley and mountains along the way. From the station, Hotel Regina is a short walk through the village.
