Activities

Discover Wengen's Wonderland

At Hotel Regina, every day is an invitation to explore the beauty of the Swiss Alps. Whether you're looking for excitement or relaxation, the stunning backdrop of the Jungfrau Mountains offers the perfect setting for unforgettable outdoor adventures. From invigorating hikes and scenic trails to more exhilarating experiences that will leave you breathless, there’s something for everyone.

At Hotel Regina, adventure and relaxation coexist, offering the perfect balance for every traveler.

Skiing & Snowboarding

Wengen is part of the Jungfrau Ski Region, boasting over 200 km of world-class slopes, becoming one of the most renowned ski regions in Switzerland. Whether you’re a seasoned skier or just starting, the variety of runs ensures there's something for everyone.

For the ultimate adrenaline rush, don’t miss the chance to ski the Lauberhorn downhill—the longest and one of the most thrilling in the world. It’s an unforgettable experience, framed by the majestic peaks of the Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau

Sledding

Experience the excitement of sledding on one of Wengen's many toboggan runs! Sled down from Wengernalp or Männlichen, two of the most popular routes, and let gravity pull you along scenic paths framed by snow-laden trees and the towering Alps.

Fun for both kids and adults, sledding is the perfect way to laugh your way through winter in Wengen. It’s an activity that brings out the childlike joy in everyone.

Ice Skating & Curling

For those seeking a bit of elegance or competition, head to our charming village ice rink. Whether you're spinning gracefully on the ice or giving curling a try, it’s an activity that can be both relaxing and engaging.

It’s the perfect place to create beautiful winter memories with friends or family while enjoying the crisp mountain air.

Winter Hiking

For a more peaceful adventure, immerse yourself in nature on one of Wengen's scenic winter hiking trails. Wander through serene forests draped in snow, taking in the panoramic views of the surrounding peaks.

The quiet beauty of the trails offers a perfect escape, where the only sounds are the crunch of snow beneath your boots and the occasional bird song. Whether it’s a short stroll or a longer trek, winter hiking in Wengen lets you experience the Alps at their most tranquil.

Snowshoeing

For those looking to explore the less-traveled paths, snowshoeing is a wonderful way to venture into the quieter corners of the Alps.

With guided tours available, you can safely explore untouched landscapes, venturing deep into the winter wilderness. It’s a meditative and refreshing way to connect with nature, perfect for those seeking a more personal encounter with the mountains.

CALL US
Jungfraujoch
Top of Europe

No winter trip to Wengen is complete without a journey to Jungfraujoch, the “Top of Europe.” Board the highest railway in Europe and ascend to 3,454 meters for a once-in-a-lifetime experience.

Once at the top, marvel at the panoramic views of snow-covered peaks, explore the magical Ice Palace, and enjoy the thrill of year-round snow. This adventure is not just about reaching the highest point—it’s about witnessing the majesty of the Swiss Alps like never before.

Hiking

Wengen is a hiker’s paradise, with over 500 kilometers of hiking trails meandering through lush meadows, dense forests, and breathtaking mountain vistas.

Popular hikes include the Männlichen to Kleine Scheidegg trail, offering spectacular views of the Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau peaks. For a more leisurely walk, try the Panorama Trail, which provides stunning views of the Lauterbrunnen Valley.

CALL US
Jungfraujoch
Top of Europe

Hop on the Jungfraubahn and head up to the Top of Europe, the highest railway station in Europe. At 3,454 meters, the Jungfraujoch offers year-round snow, an Ice Palace, and jaw-dropping views of the Aletsch Glacier, the longest glacier in the Alps.

This iconic excursion is perfect for a summer day adventure.

Mountain Biking

Wengen’s network of mountain biking trails is perfect for riders of all skill levels. Whether you're looking for a leisurely ride through the scenic valleys or a more challenging route up the mountain trails, Wengen provides the perfect terrain.

Take the Grindelwald-Männlichen trail for an unforgettable ride through alpine pastures, or try the Lauberhorn Trail for a more intense experience.

CALL US
Paragliding

For the thrill-seekers, paragliding in Wengen is a bucket-list adventure. Take off from the Männlichen or other mountain tops and experience the rush of soaring above lush valleys and towering peaks.

The breathtaking bird’s-eye view of Wengen and the surrounding Jungfrau region is an experience like no other.

CALL US
Via Ferrata

For those with a taste for adventure, the Via Ferrata from Mürren to Gimmelwald is an exciting way to experience the Swiss Alps up close.

This fixed-rope climbing route offers spectacular views of the Lauterbrunnen Valley, with sections that take you along dramatic cliffs. Guided tours are available for those new to Via Ferrata climbing.

CALL US
Trummelbach Falls

Just a short distance from Wengen, the Trummelbach Falls are a series of impressive waterfalls hidden within the cliffs of the Lauterbrunnen Valley.

These are the only glacier waterfalls in Europe accessible underground and provide a cool, refreshing stop during a summer day.
