Responsible Social Action

Make the difference for our planet

At Hotel Regina we’re committed to sustainability and community, creating meaningful impact through mindful choices that benefit our guests and environment.

Kilometer Zero

We are committed to making a positive impact on the environment while delivering exceptional quality. That’s why we focus on sourcing seasonal, locally produced ingredients, ensuring not only the freshest flavors but also a reduced environmental footprint. By choosing products carefully and working with sustainable suppliers, we’re dedicated to doing our part for the planet.

Reduce Waste

When you dine with us, you’re supporting our vision of a more sustainable future. At Jack’s Brasserie, we’ve made impactful changes that significantly reduce our laundry and water consumption. By removing table linens and investing in a redesign, we’ve managed to save 12.6 tons of laundry annually, conserving over 50,000 liters of water and reducing detergent use by nearly a ton each year. This also helps cut down electricity use by 10,815 kW annually.

Conserve Resources

To further reduce our ecological footprint, we’ve installed a special filtration system that turns local mountain spring water into natural or sparkling water, served in our restaurant. This innovation has allowed us to reduce transport miles, saving 960 kilometers of trucking each year, while cutting diesel consumption and emissions. These efforts help us minimize waste and lower costs, so we can provide sustainable drinking water in our dining spaces.

Fait Maison

Excellent home-made cuisine

The Hotel Regina Wengen proudly bears the "Fait Maison" label, which stands for home-made, freshly prepared dishes. We only use carefully selected ingredients and do not use industrially processed products.

Our cuisine focuses on regional, seasonal ingredients and offers an authentic culinary experience that emphasises quality and craftsmanship.

Savour the true taste of home-made excellence!

Swisstainable

Sustainability at Hotel Regina Wengen – Our Contribution with Swisstainable Level I

Hotel Regina in Wengen is proud to be part of the Swisstainable program and has achieved Level I – Committed. This recognized label reflects our active commitment to responsible, future-oriented tourism, in harmony with nature, the environment, and the local community.

What does Swisstainable mean?
Swisstainable is more than just a sustainability label – it is a promise. A promise to protect and preserve the unique natural beauty of Switzerland for future generations. From majestic mountains to crystal-clear lakes and lush forests, Switzerland captivates with its untouched beauty – and we are doing our part to ensure it stays that way.

By participating in the Swisstainable program, we are committed to sustainable practices across all areas of our hotel operations. For you as a guest, this means a relaxing stay where environmental consciousness and comfort go hand in hand.

Experience Sustainability – at Hotel Regina Wengen!
