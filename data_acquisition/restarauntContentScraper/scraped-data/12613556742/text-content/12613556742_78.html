Services

The Hotel Regina is a hidden gem in the heart of Wengen, a picturesque town closed off from car traffic and only accessible by train. The hotel occupies a magnificent Victorian-style building dating back to 1894, which exudes history and charm.

Guests can choose between two high-class restaurants. The first offers delicious à la carte and tasting menus, while the second offers a variety of traditional and international dishes, as well as daily meals and a selection of fine wines. After dinner, you can relax in the spacious lobby with a fireplace or enjoy a drink at the piano bar.

The hotel rooms offer breathtaking panoramic views of the Jungfrau region's mountains, the Lauterbrunnen Valley and the village of Wengen. The suites, in particular, offer a luxurious and comfortable ambience, perfect for those seeking an unforgettable stay.

For a moment of relaxation and well-being, the hotel offers a dedicated wellness area with sauna and hammam. This space is ideal for rejuvenating after a day spent exploring the surrounding natural wonders.

The Hotel Regina represents the perfect fusion of tradition and luxury, offering a unique experience in the heart of the Swiss Alps.

Step into the warmth and charm of Hotel Regina, where our family has been welcoming guests since 1894.

Nestled in the peaceful, car-free village of Wengen, we offer more than just a place to stay. With breathtaking views, cozy comforts, and a friendly atmosphere, we make you feel right at home.
