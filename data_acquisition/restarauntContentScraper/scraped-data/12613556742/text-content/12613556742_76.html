Suites

Luxury and elegance

Each of our suites offers a sanctuary of comfort, perfect for those seeking a truly unforgettable stay in the heart of the Swiss Alps. With breathtaking views of the majestic Jungfrau mountains, our suites are designed to provide you with a warm and sophisticated atmosphere. Each suite has its own unique charm, blending modern amenities with the character of our historic hotel.

With personalized services, balconies overlooking stunning alpine landscapes, and luxurious touches like elegant bathrooms and cozy lounge spaces, your stay will feel both intimate and indulgent.
