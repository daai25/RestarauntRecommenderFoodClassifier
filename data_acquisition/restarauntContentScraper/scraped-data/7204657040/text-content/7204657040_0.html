About Gaijin Izakaya

Gaijin is an homage to a Japanese Izakaya in Zurich’s Kreis 4, serving contemporary food inspired by Japanese preparation methods and flavours but encapsulating influences from all over Asia and other parts of the world. Using a traditional charcoal grill from Japan, at Gaijin we push forward modern ideas while celebrating both local but also unique ingredients from abroad, alongside beverages that mirror our cuisine.

Our approach and passion for unpretentious and delicious food accompanied by genuinely friendly service have helped us receive 14 Gault & Millau points, as well as a spot in the Michelin guide as a ‘Bib Gourmand’ restaurant, in just over two years of operation.
