Restaurant & Castle Courtyard

Situated at the peak of Thun’s Schlossberg mountain, Restaurant & Hotel Schlossberg Thun pampers its guests with freshly prepared dishes from the Alpine region.

Inspired by Alpine cuisine, our chefs work their magic on unusual lunch offers as well as à la carte or individually composed menus accompanied by matching regional wines. All dishes are served in the intimate, charming and stylish ambience of Restaurant & Hotel Schlossberg Thun.

Spend some relaxing time together at the castle. The courtyard is the perfect stopover for town hikers and day-trippers. Take some time out and sample our excellent cuisine!
