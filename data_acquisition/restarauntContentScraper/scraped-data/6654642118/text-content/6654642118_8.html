    APÉROS, EVENTS, COMPANY DINNERS OR PRIVATE PARTIES 

    The restaurant Löwenzorn with its historical ambience offers the perfect setting for aperitifs, receptions, banquets, seminars, company or private dinners and unforgettable wedding parties.

    The unique premises are at your disposal:

    • during the day (from 2-50 persons)

    • in the evening by appointment for aperitifs, receptions and banquets (from 10 to 200 people)

    ​

    LION HALL

    The wall paintings of the Löwensaal by the artist Domo Löw on the subject of Basel carnival an Basel guilds characterise this room.

    ​

    Area 100 sqm

    80 seats

    Unseated up to 70 persons

    GUILD HALL

    The stucco ceiling, the chandelier and the fireplace of the Guild Hall date from the 18th century.

    ​

    Area 50 sqm

    35 seats

    Unseated up to 50 persons

    INTARSIA HALL

    This beautifully preserved 16th century ceremonial hall is decorated with opulent Renaissance panelling.

    ​

    Area 50 sqm

    50 seats

    Unseated up to 70 persons

    DRUM- AND FENCING HALL

    The Drum- and Fancing Hall is the meeting place of the Wednesday Society, one of Basel's oldest carnival societies, wich has been in existence since 1907 and whose favourite pub has been Löwenzorn since 1922.

    ​

    Area 50 sqm

    35 seats

    Unseated up to 50 persons
