MORE THAN YOUR REGULAR BAR

Enjoy true Italian aperitivo with friends & family at our Apéro Bar in the heart of Zurich - daily from 17.00 until 20.00.​

 

Great coffee. Finest Italian cuisine. Best Apéro. Authentic Bottega.

​​

Our approach to delivering only the finest service to our customers starts and ends with our constant desire to exceed expectations. Come join us to see what we’re all about.

JUST LIKE IN ITALY...

Located just a few minutes walk from Stauffacher, our Apéro Bar is conveniently located in the heart of Zurich.

​​

An intimate, laidback locale where you can catch up over a shared plate of delicious and authentic Italian delicacies. Il Salotto brings classic Italian culture to Zurich where you can enjoy your favourite classic cocktails and food.

​​

Our aperitivo is offered from 17.00 – 20.00. Simply order your drinks and your aperitivo platter is included.
