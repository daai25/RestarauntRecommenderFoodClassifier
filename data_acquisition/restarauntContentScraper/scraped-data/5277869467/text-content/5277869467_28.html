APERITIVO BAR

In a Spritzy State of Mind...

Located just a few minutes walk from Stauffacher, our Apéro Bar is conveniently located in the heart of Zurich.​

 

An intimate, laidback locale where you can catch up over a shared plate of delicious and authentic Italian delicacies. Il Salotto brings classic Italian culture to Zurich where you can enjoy your favourite classic cocktails and food.

​​

Our aperitivo is offered from 17.00 – 20.00. Simply order your drinks and your aperitivo platter is included.

Authentic Italian Flavors

Enjoy an authentic taste of Italy with every bite. Our Aperitivo platters feature the finest selection of Italian delicacies, from aged cheeses to artisanal salumi, crafted to pair perfectly with your favorite drink.

Classic Cocktails, Reimagined

Savor timeless Italian cocktails made with care. From the iconic Negroni to the refreshing Aperol Spritz, our drinks bring Italian mixology to life, served in an ambiance that feels like home.

Aperitivo Included

​Experience the true essence of Italian aperitivo culture. Order any drink from our menu—whether it’s a refreshing spritz, a fine wine, or one of our classic cocktails—between 17:00 and 20:00, and enjoy a delicious aperitivo platter included with your drink. It’s the perfect pairing, on us.
