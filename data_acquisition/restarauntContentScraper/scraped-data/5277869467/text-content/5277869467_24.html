PRIVATE EVENTS

Host Your Private Event with Il Salotto...

Whether you’re celebrating a special occasion or hosting a corporate gathering, Il Salotto offers the perfect setting for your private event. With our Lokal available for exclusive use and the option to cater at your chosen location on a case-by-case basis, we’re here to make your vision a reality.

 

Private events are typically held on Sundays or Mondays, and we provide a full-service experience including lunch, dinner, aperitivo, and drinks. Let’s discuss your needs and craft an unforgettable event together.

Tailored Experiences

From intimate dinners to grand celebrations, our team works closely with you to customize the menu, ambiance, and details, ensuring your event reflects your personal style and preferences.

Exceptional Food & Drinks

Whether it’s a multi-course lunch, an elegant dinner, or a vibrant aperitivo hour, our menus are designed to impress. From classic cocktails to fine Italian wines, every sip and bite will leave your guests delighted.

Flexible Locations

Our cozy and stylish Lokal provides the ideal backdrop for any event, but we also offer off-site catering for select occasions. Let us know your location, and we’ll work to accommodate your needs wherever possible.

Tell us about your event—desired date, type of gathering, location preferences, and any specific ideas you have in mind. Our team will review your details and get in touch to create a tailored plan that makes your event truly special.
