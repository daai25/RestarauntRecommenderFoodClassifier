Join us for great food

Our restaurant and take away is conveniently located in the station Altstetten

We have a dedicated area with a food counter, where you can order food to take away immediately or consume it in our spacious seating area. We also have a full-service restaurant, where we cook your favourite Asian dishes just the way you like it. Also enjoy our lovely terrace in the summer.
