Seminars & Banquets in Baden

Are you looking for a seminar room or do you want to rent a room? Our hotel has a versatile room offer which is ideal for business events or private celebrations in a peaceful atmosphere. In addition to a professional consulting and organization, you can benefit from an additional banqueting and catering services.

The variety of our rooms makes it possible to hold events and meetings of various types and sizes. All our rooms are multifunctional, without pillars, have natural daylight and are available for half or full day events, depending on the occasion. Room rental fee includes Wi-Fi, projection screen, projector, a flip chart, notepad and ballpoint pens for all participants, as well as mineral water.
