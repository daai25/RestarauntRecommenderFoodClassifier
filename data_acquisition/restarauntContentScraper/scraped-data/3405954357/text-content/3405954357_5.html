Privacy Policy

Your trust is important to us, therefore we take our privacy policy and the safety of data seriously. Visiting our website

While visiting our website our server temporarily saves an access protocol. The following technical data is thereafter saved, as with all connections to a webserver, without your assistance and saved until it is automatically deleted from our server after 24 months.

  • The IP-Address of the used computer
  • The name of the owner of the IP address (internet access provider)
  • Date and time of access
  • The website from which the access was obtained (Referrer URL) with the search term if relevant.
  • The name and URL of the accessed document
  • The status-code (for example error report)
  • The operating software of the computer
  • The browser used to access (Type, version and language)
