About us

About us

We immerse our customers in royal Indian culinary delights at the Darbar Restaurant.

At Darbar Restaurant we invite you to discover the fascinating world of Indian cuisine and experience the royal hospitality of ancient India. “Darbar” means “court” or “royal palace” and we want you to feel like a valued guest at the royal court. Our restaurant is an ode to the rich Indian culture and its culinary traditions. Every detail of our interior has been carefully designed to reflect the splendor and grandeur of a royal palace. From opulent chandeliers and ornate wall paintings to elegant table decorations, we have created a unique atmosphere that will transport you back to India’s past.

Since 2017

The Beginning of an iconic Indian Restaurant

2017

The foundation

The formative ideas of setting up a state-of-the-art restaurant began in 2017 with the vision to offer authentic high quality fresh food. The founder, Ashfaq Ahmed opened the doors of the culinary haven to delight people with the best of Indian food cooked in heavenly flavors. The aim has always been to create a warm and welcoming atmosphere for our customers and to provide the best of Swiss Indian gastronomy and hospitality.

2020

Record orders despite COVID-19

Despite the adverse circumstances during Covid-19, our valued guests surprised us with an incredible response.
Their support and loyalty motivated us to remain consistent with our business and overcome the challenges during the lockdown period. We were overwhelmed by the high volume of orders. Thanks to our generous customers who helped us achieve this memorable feat.

2023

Digitization

Darbar Restaurant has gone digital by updating and modernizing its website. Now, placing an order with us online has become seamless and easy.
