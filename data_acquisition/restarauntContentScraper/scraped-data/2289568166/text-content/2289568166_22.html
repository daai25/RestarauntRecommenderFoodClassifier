Business events

Are you planning a business event and looking for the perfect venue? At Darbar, we understand the importance of business events and offer you a first-class experience that will exceed your expectations.

Our premises:

Our carefully designed rooms offer an appealing blend of elegance and comfort. We have flexible room concepts that are ideal for a variety of business events – from board meetings and seminars to celebratory company anniversaries. Our rooms can be adapted as required to welcome your guests in an intimate atmosphere or for a larger gathering.

Culinary delights:

Our renowned kitchen specializes in creating gastronomic experiences that leave an impression. From delicate canapés to luxurious multi-course menus – our talented kitchen team conjures up taste experiences that will delight your guests. We are happy to take individual dietary requirements into account and adapt the menu accordingly.

Professional service:

Our dedicated team understands the demands of business events and does everything in its power to ensure that everything runs smoothly. We offer professional support in the planning and organization of your business event, from room design and technical equipment to the coordination of on-site services.

Ambience and equipment:

Our stylish rooms are complemented by tasteful interior design and modern furnishings. We offer audiovisual technology, presentation facilities and fast WLAN to ensure that your event runs without technical difficulties.

Individual advice:

Every business event is unique and we strive to make your vision a reality. Our event team is on hand to discuss all the details, answer your questions and offer solutions that perfectly match your requirements.
