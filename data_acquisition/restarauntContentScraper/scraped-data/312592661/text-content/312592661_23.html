About Us

Our restaurant History

Welcome to Saravanaa Bhavan - a culinary haven where tradition meets taste! Discover the essence of South Indian cuisine in our brand new restaurant at Limmastrasse 50 in the heart of Zurich.

Vegetarian delights Immerse yourself in a world of vegetarian delights, meticulously prepared with the freshest ingredients and authentic South Indian spices. From spicy curries to crispy dosas, our menu offers a symphony of flavours to tantalise your taste buds.

Global heritage Saravanaa Bhavan has earned a global reputation for providing an unparalleled dining experience. Now we bring our rich culinary heritage to Zurich, inviting you to savour the authenticity and passion in every bite.

Warm ambience Step into our inviting space where the warmth of South Indian hospitality meets contemporary comfort. Whether you're gathering with friends, family or colleagues, our restaurant provides the perfect setting for memorable moments.
