Specials

At Güterhof in Schaffhausen, you can not only enjoy fine food every day and a weekly Sunday brunch. It also hosts special culinary events such as the Blauburgunder Gourmet Festival in May. You can also look forward to our popular musical events "Tanznacht40" (September to May) and "Notturno" (November to March).

Sunday brunch

Experience the perfect start to your Sunday at Güterhof in Schaffhausen with our sumptuous brunch à discrétion. Look forward to fine and regional bread specialties, a large selection of cold and hot breakfast classics and delicious desserts.


Every Sunday from 10:00 - 14:00
