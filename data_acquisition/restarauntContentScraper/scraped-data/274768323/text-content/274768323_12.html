Sunday brunch in Schaffhausen

Experience the perfect start to your Sunday at Güterhof in Schaffhausen with our sumptuous brunch à discrétion. Look forward to fine and regional bread specialties, a large selection of cold and hot breakfast classics and delicious desserts.

Every Sunday from 10:00 - 14:00
additional: Friday, 1 August, Swiss National Day (special brunch)

If the weather is nice and warm, we will be happy to reserve a table for you on the terrace directly on the banks of the Rhine. Limited places, subject to availability.

A sparkling start to the Sunday

We welcome you with a glass of Güterhof Prosecco. You can help yourself to your heart's content at our large buffet with hot and cold breakfast specialties from the Schaffhausen region. Popular meat and fish specialties are just as much a part of it as pasta. Of course, coffee, tea and fruit juices are also available.

Reservations

Please provide us with the following information when making your reservation:

  • Number of people (incl. children with age information)
  • Approximate arrival time (our recommendation: between 10.00 and 11.00 a.m.)
  • Cell phone number

If the weather is nice and warm, we will be happy to reserve a table for you on the terrace directly on the banks of the Rhine (limited seating, subject to availability). For organizational reasons, no seating requests can be considered in advance (windows or similar). 

We look forward to your reservation.
