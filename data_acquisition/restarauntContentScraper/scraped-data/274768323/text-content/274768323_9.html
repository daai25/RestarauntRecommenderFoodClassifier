Güterhof Hall Schaffhausen

We recommend our beautiful Güterhof Hall for your private event. With its up to 250-year-old wooden columns and beamed ceiling, it perfectly combines tradition and modernity. A versatile inner courtyard adjoins the hall. This offers space for up to 100 guests. The inner courtyard can be shared or rented exclusively on request.
There is no rent for the hall. There is only a minimum consumption.

Güterhof is located directly on the Rhine (Schifflände), about 15 minutes' walk from Schaffhausen railroad station. Parking is available in the immediate vicinity.

Key data Güterhof Hall

  • Capacity: up to 85 people
  • Size: 116 m²
  • Infrastructure: projector, WLAN, music system with loudspeakers, flip chart
  • Additional technology on request
  • Minimum consumption: CHF 2 000.
  • No room hire

Key data courtyard

The versatile inner courtyard can be hired for an event or in combination with an event in the Güterhof Hall.

  • Up to 100 people
  • 70 m²; covered, self-contained, not heated
  • High tables, bar tables and/or bar
  • Projector with iPod connection, screen, WLAN, flip chart
  • Ideal for aperitifs, stand-up lunches, receptions, buffets, cocktail workshops
  • CHF 1'500.00 all-inclusive rental

In combination with your event in the Güterhof hall , further activities can be held in the inner courtyard, e.g:

  • Party (dance floor & bar)
  • Workshops for groups (team building, work stations for seminars)
  • Live performances, e.g. piano session
