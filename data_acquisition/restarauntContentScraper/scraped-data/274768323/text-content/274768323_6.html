Arrival

Public transportation (train & boat)

From Schaffhausen railroad station you can reach Güterhof by bus no. 6 (direction Buchthalen) to the bus stop "Schaffhausen Schifflände" (3 min.)

It takes about 15 minutes on foot (walk through the old town of Schaffhausen).

Güterhof is located directly at the Schifflände Schaffhausen, the starting and finishing point of the scheduled boats. These operate daily from April to October between Schaffhausen and Constance/Kreuzlingen.

Timetable for Untersee and Rhine shipping

Car

From Zurich or Stuttgart, take the A4 to the Schaffhausen-Süd exit, then head towards the center. Follow the Rhine - you will find us in the first building after the Feuerthal Rhine bridge.

Parking garages:

  • Schifflände parking garage, directly behind the Güterhof (CHF 2.50/hour)
  • Sommerlust parking lot (CHF 5.- whole day), 3 min. walk to Güterhof

Visitors with reduced mobility

If you are limited in your mobility due to a disability or your age, or if you are traveling with a stroller, you can find some accessibility information here.

We are part of the OK:GO initiative. The OK:GO initiative aims to ensure that all tourism providers in Switzerland make information about the accessibility of their offer available to the public. Find out more about the OK:GO initiative.
