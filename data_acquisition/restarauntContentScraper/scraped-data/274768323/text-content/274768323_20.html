Rooms

Knights' Hall

Celebrate your company or private event in the magnificent Knights' Hall.

Castello

The Castello daytime restaurant is the former banner hall of the castle. A cozy open fire blazes in the background and you can enjoy relaxed conversations in the cozy lounge. Enjoy your lunch on the restaurant's large terrace in fine weather.

Bleuler Hall

Dine in style in a historic ambience. The Bleuler Hall is ideal for parties of up to 44 people.

Round tower

A total of 17 seats and a small balcony with a view of the Rhine await you for an inspiring seminar.

Castle parlor

In the castle parlor, which is completely made of wood, you can eat and hold meetings in a historical ambience.

Laufenstube

The modern, bright Laufenstube is small and elegant: Reserve the elegant, discreetly furnished and quiet room with a maximum of 14 seats for your company. Discuss important matters in suitable surroundings and celebrate in an intimate setting.

Wine cellar

These walls breathe history: the wine cellar at Schloss Laufen am Rheinfall is 500 years old. Here we nurture and care for more than 48 exquisite wines from the Schaffhausen region and the Zurich wine country.

Terrace

The terrace with 180 seats is wonderfully secluded.

Inner courtyard

Round off your conference or seminar with an aperitif in the castle courtyard.
