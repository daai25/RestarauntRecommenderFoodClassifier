Four decades of Suan Long

And since then, many exciting chapters have been written

Founded by a family of Chinese refugees from the Cambodian civil war, the first Suan Long Restaurant opened its doors in Zurich in 1987. With our vast array of traditional and contemporary dishes and friendly service, Suan Long has become synonymous with authentic Asian food. Our food is inspired by our family recipes and includes Chinese, Cambodian, Vietnamese and Thai cuisine and is still run today as a family business.

For four decades, Suan Long has strived to set the standard for Asian food in Zurich.
We had the privilege of serving generations of customers and won the hearts of gourmands and casual diners alike.
Our values are reflected in our food: we offer high-quality, freshly made and authentic traditional as well as contemporary dishes from China, Cambodia, Vietnam and Thailand. We use only Swiss Chicken, Pork and Beef and try to source locally as much as possible.
Suan Long is a social space where we want to connect customers with friends to share the food and drinks they love in an inviting environment. Just as our vast array of dishes, our restaurants designs reflect how far Suan Long has come with different interior styles perfect for all kind of occasions.
