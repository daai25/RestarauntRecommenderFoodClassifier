    SUSHI FUNE's Nigiri Sushi and Tuna Rolls are fresh and healthy options. Sushi contains fish with high protein and low-fat content, as well as vegetables such as avocado, cucumber, asparagus, and cabbage. These ingredients contain vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants that are beneficial for maintaining good health. In addition, sushi rice made with rice and vinegar is believed to have a positive effect on intestinal health and can help regulate gut microbiota.

     

    Tuna is known for containing high-quality fatty acids such as DHA and EPA, which can support healthy brain and heart functions. Sushi also includes nutrient-rich ingredients such as avocado, cucumber, and nori, which are known to be beneficial for improving skin and immunity. Sushi is rich in protein, vitamins, and minerals, particularly omega-3 fatty acids, which are beneficial for heart and brain health. The condiments such as nori, wasabi, and ginger also have health benefits.
