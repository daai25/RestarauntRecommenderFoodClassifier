Eritrean and Ethiopian Specialities

Enjoy culinary food and drinks from Eritrea and Ethiopia in a pleasant and friendly atmosphere. Our restaurant is entirely dedicated to East African specialties. In our traditionally furnished rooms in Zurich you will find a variety of delicacies from Eritrean and Ethiopian cuisine. Come to our coffee ceremony too. You have certainly never experienced a coffee experience like this. We are looking forward to your visit!
