Large double room with views of the lake

Room No. 34

2 GUESTS

21 qm

CHF 79 - 109.-/night + cleaning fee + tourist tax

BOOK NOW

From this room you have a magnificent view of Lake Thun and the mountains.

The non-smoking room has two single beds, parquet flooring and a modern bathroom with shower, sink and toilet. Towels and hair dryer are also provided. The room has WiFi, but no TV.

Pets are not allowed in this room.

In this room you treat yourself and other guests to a special portion of peace and quiet.

 

The quiet floor: Rooms 30-35 are specifically available for guests seeking peace and quiet. We ask you to refrain from making noise in the hallway, to close doors quietly and to maintain the room volume in the room.
Extra beds or baby cots are not possible on this floor. We are happy to accommodate guests with children in rooms 40-45.
