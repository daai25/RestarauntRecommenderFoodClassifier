Business

Focus and inspiration for your business

Z Aeschiried is as individual as your business event. We will be happy to put together a suitable offer for you.

OFFER HOUSE Z HOTEL

You are looking for …

… a place where the possibilities are as individual as your business? And this at a location that inspires, creates new perspectives and yet can be reached quickly?

Are you looking for the ideal location for a multi-day teambuilding event or a workation? Are you planning an offsite, a retreat, a seminar or remote work? Then you have come to the right place.

 

We are …

… a retreat hotel with a magnificent view of Lake Thun and the mountains of the Bernese Oberland. The fantastic view creates focus, inspiration and new perspectives. At the same time, the idyllic location offers the best conditions to relax after work and to share experiences as a team. We can be reached from Bern in only 40min.

What we offer …

The beautiful location of the Retreat Hotel Z Aeschiried offers relaxation and inspiration at the same time.

 

As a social enterprise, it is also our vision to meet different social needs. Thus, with your stay you contribute, among other things, to creating jobs for people in challenging situations.

 

In other ways, too, we focus on what is important to you – and thus offer you affordable prices.
As a small hotel business, we do not have a permanently staffed reception desk, but are happy to welcome you personally and are always available to answer your questions.
Also, we do not clean the guest rooms daily. Instead, you get a price reduction from two nights.

 

Your stay with us is as individual as your business and as meaningful as you have always wanted.

Our catering offer

Z Morge-Buffet (Bernese German for «breakfast buffet») is included in the price of the overnight stay: 

A regional breakfast buffet awaits you.

 

Z Mittag – Lunch (when booking full board):

Our simple daily Lunch. Additionally with salad, bread and butter.
Uncomplicated. Balanced. Sometimes vegetarian, sometimes with meat.

 

Z Nacht (Bernese German for «dinner») when booking half board:

Our daily Dinner in 3 courses: Salad, main course and dessert.
Uncomplicated favorite recipes. Sometimes vegetarian and sometimes with meat. Always with that certain something.

 

Would you like a special group experience?
We would be happy to offer you our outdoor fondue or barbecue buffet.
A unique group experience with a fantastic view.

 

It is not possible to prepare or consume food or drinks that you have brought yourself.

 

Your well-being is our priority. If our catering offer is not suitable for you, we will be happy to inform you about other restaurants in the vicinity.

House «Z Huus»

The House «Z Huus» offers the possibility of an exclusive booking and thus a lot of space and privacy for your group. In addition to the large room Z Stockhorn, your booking also includes the seminar room Z Niederhorn.

Seminar room Z Niederhorn

The seminar room Z Niederhorn has a magnificent view of the garden and the mountains. It also offers three individual workstations where the most important things for work can be done in between. If required, it can be connected to the large room Z Stockhorn.

Ideal for:

  • workshops
  • offsites
  • workstations
  • seminars

Seating: flexible

Area: 10m x 5.30m

Equipment (on request/for a fee):

  • Fixed and mobile whiteboard
  • Screen
  • Beamer
  • Flipcharts
  • Moderation case
  • 3 individual workstations
  • Coffee station

Large hall Z Stockhorn

With every booking of the House «Z Huus» the hall Z Stockhorn as a lounge is included. This offers a magnificent view of the garden, Lake Thun and the mountains. It has a lounge area to linger and direct access to the terrace. The Z Stockhorn room can be connected to the Z Niederhorn seminar room if required.

Ideal: as a versatile lounge for groups

Seating: flexible

Area: 13m x 10.50m

Equipment: on request

 

More information about the group kitchen.

House «Z Hotel»

The House «Z Hotel» awaits you with modern rooms and the magnificent Z Panorama room. This is exclusively available for your group, while the rest of the house normally also accommodates other guests. The entire House «Z Hotel» is available for exclusive use only upon request and when booking all 12 rooms.

Seminar room Z Panorama

The seminar room Z Panorama inspires with large window fronts and the best view. It can be divided into 2 parts and has direct access to the terrace.

Ideal for:

  • seminars
  • presentation meetings
  • strategy days

Seating: flexible

Total area: 4.50m x 14.80m

Area divided: rear part: 4.50m x 6.50m; middle part: 4.50m x 4.30m

Equipment (on request/for a fee):

  • screen
  • projector
  • flipchart
  • mobile whiteboard
  • presentation case
  • coffee station

Accommodation - House «Z Hotel»

The modern House «Z Hotel» has 12 rooms, all with shower/WC. 10 of the rooms have a beautiful view of Lake Thun and the mountains. On request and subject to availability, a floor of 6 rooms or the whole House «Z Hotel» can be booked exclusively.

The rooms in the House «Z Hotel» are suitable in combination with the seminar room Z Panorama below.
