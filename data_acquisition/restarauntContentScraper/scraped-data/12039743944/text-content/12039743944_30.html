Groups

Our group accommodation is available for seminars or group trips. Rooms with a view like that are guaranteed to create new perspectives.

Our group accommodation consists of 14 simple double rooms with shower/WC. 4 rooms are furnished with two extra beds in a gallery, which are ideal for children or uncomplicated guests. This way we can accommodate groups from 8 up to 36 people on one floor. On request, additional rooms can be booked in the other building.

Eight rooms of the group accommodation have a balcony and beautiful views of Lake Thun and the mountains surrounding the area. Six rooms have a small private seating area on the hillside.

Our regional Z Morge-Buffet (Bernese German for «breakfast buffet») is included in the overnight price. Upon request, groups can additionally book our half or full board.

 

The preparation or consumption of self-brought food and beverages is not possible.

 

Seminar and recreation rooms can be booked on request.

Our catering offer

Z Morge-Buffet (Bernese German for «breakfast buffet») is included in the price of the overnight stay: 

A regional breakfast buffet awaits you.

 

Z Mittag – Lunch (when booking full board):

Our simple daily Lunch. Additionally with salad, bread and butter.
Uncomplicated. Balanced. Sometimes vegetarian, sometimes with meat.

 

Z Nacht (Bernese German for «dinner») when booking half board:

Our daily Dinner in 3 courses: Salad, main course and dessert.
Uncomplicated favorite recipes. Sometimes vegetarian and sometimes with meat. Always with that certain something.

 

Would you like a special group experience?
We would be happy to offer you our outdoor fondue or barbecue buffet.
A unique group experience with a fantastic view.

 

It is not possible to prepare or consume food or drinks that you have brought yourself.

 

Your well-being is our priority. If our catering offer is not suitable for you, we will be happy to inform you about other restaurants in the vicinity.
