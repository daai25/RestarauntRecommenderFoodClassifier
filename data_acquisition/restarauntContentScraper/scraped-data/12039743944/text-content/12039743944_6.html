Restaurant

We are pleased to welcome you in our restaurant «Z Reschti». In a cozy atmosphere and with the best view of Lake Thun and the mountains.

Reservations

Our à la carte offer will not take place until further notice for personnel reasons. We recommend that external guests reserve their table for Z Morge (Breakfast) in advance by telephone. We look forward to your call on 079 292 30 00.

Opening hours for hotel guests: 

 

As a hotel guest, you can look forward to our regional Z Morge (Bernese German for «breakfast») every day and, on request, a fine, regional Z Nacht (Bernese German for «dinner») every day.

 

The Z Nacht takes place in the form of an individually and lovingly prepared Z Nacht-Chischtli (Bernese German for «little dinner box»). You can choose from a variety of hot main courses, soups, salads and desserts. All dishes are home-made with products from the region.

 

You can order and pay this at the Z Morge for the same evening. Our team will be happy to give you more detailed information.

 

Alternatively, you can pre-order the Z Nacht-Chischtli here. The latest time to order is 10 a.m. for the same evening:

Z Nacht-Chischtli:

 

Our à la carte menu will not be available until further notice due to staff shortages.

 

Instead, we look forward to spoiling you with a delicious regional Z Nacht in the form of the Z Nacht-Chischtli.

Opening hours for external guests:

 

Z Morge (Bernese German for «breakfast»):

Monday to Sunday: 8:00-10:00 a.m.

 

During the day:

Our self-service kiosk with homemade snacks and drinks is open all day for you.

 

 

Z Nacht (Bernese German for «dinner»):

We are happy to prepare our lovingly designed Z Nacht-Chischtli for you.

This can be ordered by 10 a.m. using the order form. We will then be waiting for you at 6.30 p.m. with the finished Z Nacht-Chischtli at Z Reschti. We look forward to hosting you.

Our offer

Z Breakfast

We are happy to spoil you with our regional breakfast.
The Z Breakfast is included in the price per night for our hotel guests.

Z Lunch

Most of our guests are on the road during the day. Therefore, a hot lunch is currently provided only for groups and by appointment.

However, all guests can enjoy homemade snacks as well as cold and hot drinks at our self-service kiosk throughout the day.

Z Dinner

We look forward to spoiling you with a selection of home-made dishes in the form of a Z Nacht-Chischtli lovingly prepared for you.
