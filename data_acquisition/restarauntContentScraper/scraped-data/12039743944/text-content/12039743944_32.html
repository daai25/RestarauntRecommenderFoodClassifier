Quiet camper site with lake view and mountain panorama

2-5 GUESTS

50 m2

CHF 49,-/night + tourist tax

BOOK NOW

Exclusive camp for a camper with a magnificent view through a forest clearing on Lake Thun and Lake Brienz. The driveway is relatively steep (up to 17% slope) and narrow, so only campers up to 2.10m wide and 2.45m high are possible.

The camp is located on the area of the hotel “Z Aeschiried” and directly on a cow pasture. There is a table with benches, beautiful hiking trails, barbecue area and plenty of space for outdoor activities. There is a toilet and sink available.

Minimum stay: 2 nights

Max. Number of persons: 5 persons
