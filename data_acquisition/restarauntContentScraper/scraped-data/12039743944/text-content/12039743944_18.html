Where you do good while you relax.

Z Aeschiried - the Retreat Hotel in the Bernese Oberland

A retreat with a priceless view.

At an affordable price.

Whether as an individual guest, couple, group or business: With us you will enjoy a retreat with a magnificent view of Lake Thun and the mountains of the Bernese Oberland. Our quiet location offers relaxation and creates new perspectives.

At the same time The Retreat Hotel Z Aeschiried offers an ideal starting point for numerous excursions and activities around Lake Thun and in the Bernese Oberland.

ROOMS OVERVIEW

What to expect

The longer you stay with us, the cheaper your stay will be. And this already from the second night. Because compared to other hotels, your room will not be cleaned daily and this saving will directly affect your price. Our experience shows that most guests prefer a good price over daily room cleaning. We hope that this will ideally suit your needs as well.

More concrete information about our 'slightly different hotel' offer for your trip to the Bernese Oberland can be found here:

Restaurant and terrace with view

Our restaurant and the terrace offer you a beautiful view over Lake Thun and the mountains.

MORE DETAILS

No daily room cleaning

Most guests prefer a good price over daily room cleaning. Therefore, we will not clean your room daily. Instead, we will give you a significant price reduction starting from the second night.

Self-Check-In

After booking, our guests will receive all information for the arrival and an independent check-in by email. Check-in is at 16 o'clock.

Free WiFi

The WiFi with 50mbit is available to our guests free of charge. 5G reception is available on Mobile.

Free parking

Free parking spaces are available for our guests behind the house.

Non smoking

Smoking in the house and on the balconies is not allowed. Smoking is possible at the ashtrays behind and in front of the house.

Fantastic location for exploring the area

rediscover

the Bernese Oberland

With us you have an ideal starting point for hiking, biking and numerous excursions in the Bernese Oberland.

You like being outdoors? The hiking and biking trails and in winter the ski slopes and toboggan runs start right outside our door. In addition, there are numerous possibilities for barbecuing or simply enjoying the view on a panorama bench.

Would you like to explore the world-famous sights of the Bernese Oberland? Grindelwald, the world-famous Interlaken, the Jungfraujoch or the Schilthorn are within easy reach.

You prefer some secret tips? We got them for you! If you prefer to visit one of the impressive castles in the area, stroll through the beautiful old town of Thun, enjoy a brunch on the Niesen or swim in the clear waters of Lake Thun, you've come to the right place.

And after an eventful day, it's best to end the evening on the balcony or terrace and enjoy the sunset over Lake Thun and the surrounding mountains.
