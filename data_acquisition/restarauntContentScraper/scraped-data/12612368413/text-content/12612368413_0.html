Your 5-Star luxury Hotel in Switzerland

THE CHEDI ANDERMATT

Discover our Peaks

Since opening in 2013, The Chedi Andermatt has become one of the most desirable luxury resorts in the world with its casual 5-star deluxe atmosphere. Surrounded by the beauty of the Swiss Alps, this architectural masterpiece of Alpine chic combines Asian-inspired elements, authentic hospitality and classic European comfort.

At an altitude of 1,444 metres, our 5-star luxury hotel in Switzerland is the place to create unforgettable memories. 119 elegant, spacious rooms and suites, five award-winning restaurants and bars, a unique cigar and wine cellar. and a 2,400-square-metresThe Spa & Health Club make The Chedi Andermatt an inspiring place of relaxation and indulgence. 

There is also plenty to explore in the region. Whether biking, hiking, golfing, fly fishing or skiing on world-class slopes in winter, spectacular adventures await you in the Ursern valley. The Chedi Andermatt consistently receives top ratings from Forbes Travel Guide and maintains its position as one of Europe's leading hotels.

The Spa & Health Club

Dive into a world of tranquillity and relaxation at our hotel in Andermatt: experience the symbiosis of Alpine and Asian traditions in their most soothing form across 2,400 square metres.

At The Spa & Health Club, a comprehensive range awaits to harmonize body, mind, and soul. The spacious hydrotherapy area with saunas and steam baths, along with the relaxation lounge, offer pure relaxation. Enjoy the tranquillity of our impressive indoor pool or the heated outdoor pool with breathtaking views of the mountain panorama. This is complemented by a first-class fitness facility equipped by TechnoGym, which offers a variety of options to support your goals for mental strength and physical health with a variety of options.
