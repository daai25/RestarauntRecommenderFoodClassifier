A few words about us...

Welcome to our cozy corner of Swiss culinary delight! As a family-run restaurant, we take pride in serving authentic Swiss dishes that pay homage to our heritage. Our menu is filled with hearty options crafted from age-old recipes, ensuring that every bite feels like a warm hug from a loved one.
We believe that good food is best enjoyed in great company, so we’ve created a relaxed atmosphere where everyone feels at home. Our restaurant is one of the oldest in the whole town of Bern. From couples enjoying a quiet dinner to families celebrating milestones or even travel groups, we invite you to settle in and savor moments together.
When it comes to portions, we don't hold back! We want to ensure that you leave our table not just satisfied, but truly full. Whether you're indulging in Fondue, a robust Rösti or the famous Berner-Platter, you can always count on us to serve up generous helpings that nourish both body and soul.


Our friendly staff can't wait to welcome you with smiles and great service. We live by the motto that dining out should be a joyful experience that brings you closer to those around you. So come on by, roll up your sleeves, and enjoy a delicious slice of Switzerland with us! We are looking forward on meeting you.
