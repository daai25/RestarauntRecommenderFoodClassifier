    Menu Order Important Information

    Pre-Order Your Meals for Pickup – Easy and Convenient!

    We are excited to offer our customers the option to pre-order their meals to ensure availability and a seamless pickup experience. Here’s how it works and what you need to know:

    • How to Pre-Order: You can place your pre-order by phone, in person at the store, by filling-in our contact us form, via email or order online. This flexibility ensures that securing your meal is as convenient as possible.

    • Payment Options: Pre-orders require either full prepayment at the time of ordering or payment upon pickup. We accept cash, credit / debit card and TWINT. 

    • Cancellation Policy: Cancellations are accepted up until 24 hours before pickup. Beyond this period, cancellations may not be eligible for refunds.

    • No-Show Policy: If a pre-order is not picked up within the designated time, the order may be forfeited without a refund, and the meal may be offered to another customer. Customer will not be informed from us. 

    • Limited Quantities: Please note that portions are limited to ensure freshness and quality. We encourage you to pre-order early to secure your meal and avoid disappointment.

     

    This information is designed to provide you with fresh, high-quality meals while helping us manage demand effectively.
