    Wake Up to Freshly Baked Goodness

    Delicious Greek pies, snacks, and sandwiches made with love, every day.

    When we think of a traditional Greek breakfast, "Tiropita" (cheese pie) is the first thing that comes to mind—but that's just the beginning!

     

    Every day at Café - Patisserie "MÉLI", you’ll find a vast selection of freshly baked Peinirli (an elongated, open pie made in a boat shape), homemade 'Hortopita' (Greek savory pier with greens and herbs), 'Spanakotiropita' (spinach and feta cheese pie), chicken pie,  cheese-filled bagel, fluffy croissants.

     

    Indulge in delicious cakes, muffins, soft cinnamon rolls, juicy cookies, handmade bars, and yogurt topped with honey, fruits, tahini, or nuts to give your day a boost of flavor and energy.

     

    For something savory, enjoy sandwiches in irresistible combinations—all made fresh to order with high-quality ingredients.
