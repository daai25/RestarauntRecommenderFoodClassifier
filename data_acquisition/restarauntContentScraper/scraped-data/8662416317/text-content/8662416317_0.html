ABOUT US

INTERLAKEN EST.2001 - UNTERSEEN EST.2010 - GRINDELWALD EST.2015

Established in 2001, Bebbi Boesch came up with the idea that dining should not be just about great food - each individual should experience excellent entertainment while enjoying their food in the exquisite surroundings of gorgeous Switzerland. Keeping the traditional Swiss culture, Bebbi Boesch created the cozy Swiss interior of Bebbis Restaurants with Swiss paintings and traditional Swiss folklore intsruments to give tourists a glimpse of Switzerland’s rich historic culture.
Caters for thousands every year, Bebbis Restaurant is now fully decorated with very happy tourists leaving their own pieces of fun Swiss memories behind. Open 365 days a year, Bebbis Restaurant has become Interlaken’s most famous eatery which offers unforgettable memories for Honeymooners to Huge Tour Groups. Enjoy a scrumptious lunch and coffee at Bebbis Restaurant while enjoying the beauty of JungFrau. Then in the evening, feast on traditional Fondue dinner while checking out our great entertainment - alphorn, traditional folklore show.
