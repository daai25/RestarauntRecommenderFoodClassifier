Easter at Hotel Bernina 1865: An Engadine Spring AwakeningA Final Ski Run or an Enchanted Walk?

  • Hotel Bernina 1865
  • Mar 3
  • 1 min read

Choose how to experience your Easter: launch yourself onto the perfectly snow-covered slopes for a final, thrilling ski run, or immerse yourself in the tranquility of nature with a rejuvenating walk.Whatever your choice, Hotel Bernina 1865 will be your charming refuge, where warm hospitality blends with timeless elegance.

Imagine the crisp April air, the still snow-capped peaks tinged pink at dawn, and the gentle sound of horses' hooves rhythmically marking your journey.At Hotel Bernina 1865, we invite you to experience an unforgettable Easter, a perfect blend of Alpine tradition and refined luxury.

April by Carriage: A Fairytale ExperienceLet yourself be enveloped by the magic of the Engadine aboard a romantic horse-drawn carriage.Glide through postcard-perfect landscapes, where picturesque villages reflect in crystal-clear lakes, and the scent of pines fills the air.Admire the colors of spring blossoming at the foot of the majestic mountains, a true feast for the eyes.
