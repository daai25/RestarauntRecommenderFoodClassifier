Welcome to the
Cocktail Bar at
Hotel Bernina 1865

Located in the heart of the Swiss Alps, our cocktail bar offers a unique and refined experience, open to both hotel guests and external visitors. Here, elegance and conviviality blend in a welcoming and sophisticated environment.

A Refined Experience

At the Hotel Bernina 1865 cocktail bar, every detail is designed to offer you unforgettable moments. Our team of expert mixologists is ready to surprise you with a selection of classic and innovative cocktails, prepared with high-quality ingredients and unparalleled creativity.

Atmosphere and Style

Not staying with us? No problem! Our cocktail bar is also open to the public, offering everyone the opportunity to experience the exclusive atmosphere of Hotel Bernina 1865. Join us for an aperitif after a day on the slopes or an evening drink in good company.​

​

Our cocktail bar is the ideal place to relax and socialize. With elegant design and impeccable service, we offer you an oasis of tranquility where you can enjoy your favorite drink. Whether you prefer a classic Martini, an exotic Mai Tai, or a personalized cocktail, you'll find what you desire.

​​


Make your evening special by attending our exclusive events and theme nights. From whiskey tastings to jazz evenings, our calendar is filled with unmissable appointments. Discover more on our Events page and don't miss the chance to live unique experiences.

Book Your Table

Ensure your place in our renowned cocktail bar by reserving a table. Whether it's for a romantic evening, a gathering with friends, or a special event, we are ready to welcome you with dedicated service and an unforgettable atmosphere.

​

We look forward to welcoming you to the cocktail bar at Hotel Bernina 1865 to offer you an experience of taste and style like no other.
