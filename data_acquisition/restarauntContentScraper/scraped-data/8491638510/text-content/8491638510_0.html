Samedan, nestled in the heart of the Engadin valley in Switzerland, is a place where natural beauty and rich history intertwine seamlessly. This charming alpine village, with its picturesque setting surrounded by majestic mountains, crystal-clear lakes, and lush meadows, captivates visitors with its serene atmosphere and timeless allure.

At the heart of Samedan lies its rich history, dating back centuries. Originally a small farming community, Samedan evolved into a vital trading post along the ancient trade routes that crisscrossed the Alps. Its strategic location made it a hub for commerce and culture, attracting merchants, travelers, and artisans from far and wide.

One of the most notable landmarks in Samedan is the St. Peter's Church, a stunning example of Engadin architecture dating back to the 12th century. Its distinctive onion-shaped dome and ornate frescoes adorn the interior, offering a glimpse into the region's religious and artistic heritage.

As you wander through the cobbled streets of Samedan, you'll encounter elegant historic buildings, quaint cafes, and artisan shops, each bearing witness to the village's storied past. The Engadin Museum provides a fascinating insight into the local history, showcasing artifacts, photographs, and exhibits that celebrate Samedan's cultural legacy.

Beyond its historical treasures, Samedan is renowned for its natural beauty and outdoor pursuits. In the summer months, the surrounding mountains beckon hikers and climbers with their rugged trails and panoramic vistas. The Engadin Valley is also a paradise for cyclists, offering scenic routes that wind through alpine landscapes and charming villages.

In winter, Samedan transforms into a winter wonderland, attracting snow enthusiasts from around the world. The nearby slopes of Corviglia and Corvatsch offer world-class skiing and snowboarding opportunities, while the expansive network of cross-country ski trails provides a serene way to explore the snow-covered landscape.

But perhaps the true beauty of Samedan lies in its ability to enchant visitors with its timeless charm and warm hospitality. Whether you're exploring its historic streets, immersing yourself in nature, or simply savoring the tranquility of this alpine oasis, Samedan is a place that leaves an indelible mark on the heart and soul.

Explore the beauty

Nestled amidst the breathtaking beauty of the Engadine Valley lies a true gem of Swiss alpine wonder - the Bernina Express. This iconic train journey traverses some of the most spectacular landscapes in the world, offering passengers an unforgettable experience of natural splendor and engineering marvels.

As the Bernina Express winds its way through the Engadine, it unveils a panorama of snow-capped peaks, shimmering glaciers, and pristine lakes. The crisp mountain air fills the senses, invigorating the soul with each passing moment. It's a journey that transcends mere transportation, becoming a voyage of discovery through a land of timeless beauty.

One of the most captivating moments of the Bernina Express journey is when it makes its ascent up the towering Bernina Pass. Here, passengers are treated to unparalleled views of the surrounding mountains, with the Engadine Valley stretching out below like a vast, unspoiled tapestry. It's a scene of natural grandeur that leaves a lasting impression on all who behold it.

But perhaps the most enchanting part of the Bernina Express experience is when it makes its stop right below Chesa Bernina. As the train pulls into the station, guests at the holiday home are treated to a front-row seat to one of the most iconic sights in Switzerland. The vibrant red train contrasts beautifully against the backdrop of the snow-capped mountains, creating a scene straight out of a postcard.

For those staying at Chesa Bernina, the train's arrival is not just a moment of visual splendor, but also a convenient link to explore the surrounding area. Guests can hop aboard the Bernina Express and embark on a journey through the Engadine, discovering quaint villages, alpine meadows, and hidden valleys along the way.

In every season, whether blanketed in snow or bathed in summer sunshine, the beauty of the Engadine Valley never fails to captivate the heart. And with the Bernina Express passing right below Chesa Bernina, guests have the perfect vantage point to experience the magic of this enchanting alpine paradise.
