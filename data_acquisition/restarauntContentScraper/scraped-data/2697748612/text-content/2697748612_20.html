Genuine Neapolitan pizza art

The art of the Neapolitan “Pizzaiuolo” is considered UNESCO Cultural Heritage of Humanity. Our goal is to preserve and continue this heritage.

That’s why we make our pizzas strictly according to the guidelines of the Associazione Verace Pizza Napoletana. So that you can enjoy authentic Neapolitan pizza and Campanian street food here in Zurich.

Gianluca Biondo
