The neighborhood

The Erlenmatt neighborhood is a cosmos unto itself. Its history is marked by fascinating architecture and is brimming with youthful energy. In the Erlenmatt Ost district, over 500 people from all over the world call this place home. Sustainability is a key focus here. Have you ever heard of the 2000-watt society?

City & Nature

Close to a recreational area as well as the lively city, you'll find Silo. Whether on foot, by bike, tram or bus, you can reach the city center in just a few minutes. Our hostel guests can even use public transport for free thanks to the BaselCard.
