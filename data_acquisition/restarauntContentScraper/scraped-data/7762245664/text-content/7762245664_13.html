Welcome

Life is bittersweet, much like the cacao beans that were once stored where our Silo Design & Lifestyle Hostel now welcomes people from all over the world - people like you.

When you look through Silo's unique porthole-like windows, the world and its neighbourhood appear in a new light. And when the sun shines through, the shades are lowered, making it feel as if Silo is winking at you - come closer, come in!

Location

What was once a freight station is now a vibrant neighborhood - Erlenmatt. Within just a few minutes walk, you can reach Basel Badischer Bahnhof as well as the city's hotspots.
