Our Soul

Flavors of TOKYO

We place a high value on authenticity and craftsmanship. In the open kitchen, our dishes such as sushi, gyoza, ramen and donburi are freshly prepared on site under the strict eye of chef Hide Manaka.

Products

swiss & sustainable

We cook with quality Swiss meats and eggs, and all of our fish are certified by internationally renowned organizations such as ASC and MSC which ensure their sustainability. 

OUR menu

Whether it’s a bowl of hot Ramen noodle soup, sizzling homemade Gyozas, or delicate arrangements of Sushi, we’re certain you’ll notice the authentic Tokyo vibe from the first bite.
