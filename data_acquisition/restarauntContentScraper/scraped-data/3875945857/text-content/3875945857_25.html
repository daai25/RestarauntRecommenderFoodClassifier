We warmly welcome you to the Metropol Zurich.

We are pleased that you have found your way to us. We are pleased to be able to entertain and delight you in a very special place: In one of the most magnificent houses of the Belle Époque and therefore a landmark of Zurich.

The historic building is home to a restaurant with modern Japanese cuisine in a high-class atmosphere, a champagne bar, a café and two banqueting rooms with modern technology for all kinds of events.

All year round, the outdoor seating invites you to enjoy the fabulous arcades and a beautiful marble floor.

A highlight and popular meeting place to see and be seen.

We look forward to your visit.

Modern Japanese Cuisine

Modern Japanese cuisine, served in Izakaya style, for a fine business lunch or dinner in a stylish ambience.
With us, the classic menu order does not apply. The dishes are meant to be shared and are served as soon as they are ready.

Café & Champagne Bar

This is where people meet in the morning for a cosy breakfast, at lunchtime for a casual lunch and at the end of the day for a convivial aperitif. In summer, the atmosphere on our terrace under the open arcades is especially beautiful.

Bar menu

Banquets & (hybrid) events at Metropol

Centrally located between Bahnhofstrasse and Limmat, we are not only in a top location, but also offer the ideal venue for occasions and events of all kinds for up to 250 people. Our rooms are equipped with state-of-the-art technology and excellent acoustics. Seminars, congresses, banquets, weddings and much more become an unforgettable experience here.
