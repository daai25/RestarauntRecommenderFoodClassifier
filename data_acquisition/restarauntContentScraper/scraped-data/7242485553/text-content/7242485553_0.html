Surrounded by the sound of the Alps

The traditional hotel in Toggenburg

Our hotel in Toggenburg is located high above the valley floor and offers unique perspectives. Between alpine meadows, Schwendisee and the seven Churfirsten peaks, we welcome teams seeking new perspectives. Couples celebrating their vows. And everyone who wants to escape the stress of everyday life. Here, nature sets the pace and tranquillity sets the tone.

 

Sounds like music to your ears? Then feel free to browse through our online home!

The first guests were already blown away some 113 years ago.

And the reasons for it have not changed to this day.

The location

Directly on the piste and close to Schwendisee.

The tranquillity

Surrounded by nature and far from the traffic.

The family

Hospitality spanning four generations

The views

All-round panoramas from Säntis to the Churfirsten.

Game and wine
Delightful meals from early to late

Our chef orchestrates his team with passion and creativity and creates a symphony of special flavours in the kitchen. Fresh, homemade and creative dishes are served in the cosy and charming parlours. And every creation can be accompanied by a fitting bottle from the wine cellar.

Water lilies and peace of mind
Relaxing by nature

Our hotel in Toggenburg not only has a wellness area, it is even located in one. Just a few steps and you can relax and dream on the lawn or swim and splash in the Schwendisee The hotel also has saunas, steam baths, whirlpool and treatments to bring body and soul back into harmony.

Find out more
Wander and wonder
Picture-perfect panoramas around every corner

After a hearty breakfast, let the call of the mountains draw you outdoors. From the hotel you can leave your car and cares behind and immerse yourself in the symphony of the Toggenburg mountains. A particularly melodic experience awaits you along the famous Klangweg, which begins just a few steps away.

The place to meet
Space and peace for big ideas

The mountains bring you a fresh wind and open up new perspectives. High above the valley, it is almost impossible not to gain new perspectives. Our five halls offer space for up to 200 people - ideal for business meetings, seminars and workshops, but also for banquets, weddings and other festivities.

Slopes and scenery
Surrounded by mountains of pistes

Stump's Alpenrose is located between the two ski areas Wildhaus and Chäserrugg. A few steps outside the door are all it takes and you're right on the pistes. The last turn often takes you up to the door. As our guest, you will also receive an advantageous special ticket for the Wildhaus ski area.
