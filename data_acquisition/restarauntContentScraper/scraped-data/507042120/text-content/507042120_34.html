Breakfast buffet

  • Daily, 07:00 AM - 10:30 AM
  • A perfect day here starts with a freshly made smoothie! A wide range of hot and cold dishes are available, and everything is absolutely fresh. Vegetarians will find good menu choices too, of course. Breakfast includes a broad variety of quality coffees and teas.

Our breakfast team looks forward to serving you, and will gladly answer any questions you may have.


Lunch & Afternoon

  • Monday - Friday 11:30 AM - 2:00 PM
  • Saturday & Sunday 11:30 AM - 11:00 PM (continuous) - Our small, exquisite menu: lunch and afternoon until 5:30 PM

Take your pick from any items on the menu, including the perennial favorite 'build your own paella' and hot and cold tapas to suit your taste.

Additionally, at lunchtime, we offer affordable daily menus and a rich salad buffet.


Dinner

  • Monday - Thursday, 6:00 PM - 11:00 PM
  • Friday 6:00 PM - 11:30 PM
  • Saturday & Sunday 11:30 AM - 11:00 PM (continuous) - Our small, exquisite menu: lunch and afternoon until 5:30 PM

You can spend unforgettable evenings in the popular BOLERO Restaurante, enjoying Spanish specialties like tapas, paellas y vinos. Explore the culinary delights on our menu along with a great selection of open wines and gins.

For us hospitality is personal and uncomplicated. We look forward to greeting you: "hasta pronto en el BOLERO Restaurante!" It is strongly recommended to make table reservations.
