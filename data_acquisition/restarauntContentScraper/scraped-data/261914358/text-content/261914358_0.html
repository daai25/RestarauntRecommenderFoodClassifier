We are moving!

After 3 beautiful years at Gasometerstrasse 5, the DAR team is relocating to Westrasse 20 in Wiedikon.

 

The new location brings us closer to our sibling venues Restaurant KLE and WineBar COR; ergo, the family is reunited. Dar 2.0 will remain true to its original essence with a few twists to improve your experience.

 

The new location comes with an extensive “All day sun” terrace so we feel compelled to offer new opening hours and welcome you for coffee and apero throughout the day.

​

Thank you for this last 3,5 years in Kreis 5 and see you soon at Kreis 3!

About DAR

Restaurant

DAR has a sweet double meaning, in Moroccan it means "Maison", paying tribute to the past 20 years of our new home, and in Spanish it means "to give" which is the most important act we believe in besides love. 

​

Spanish and Moroccan are the two pillars of our cuisine but we take inspiration from any tasty corner of the world. The menu at DAR is created to share, with the idea that no matter our differences, we all break bread together. 

​

Everything from the food to the drinks is plant based and we are proud to say that the seasons dictate the menu changes for us.
