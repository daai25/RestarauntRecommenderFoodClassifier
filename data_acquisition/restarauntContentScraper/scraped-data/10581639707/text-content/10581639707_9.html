    RESERVATION

    CAPACITY OF UP TO 10 PERSONS (depending on seating area)

    • online: latest until 11:30 and 17:00 for lunch and dinner on the same day, respectively. maximum 90 days in advance.

    • phone: 061 311 13 13 (tue-fri: 10-14h & 17-22h, sat: 16-22h)

    • email: info@miake.ch

     

    TIME-LIMITS

    • 1 person: 1h30h

    • 2-5 persons: 2h (only at 18:00 & 18:15)​

    • 6 persons: 2h15 (only at 18:00 & 18:15)​

    • 7-10 persons: no time-limit

    • please contact us to inquire about exceptions

    ​​

    CHANGES / CANCELLATIONS

    • please contact us in case of any changes (e.g. number of people, reservation time)

    • online cancellations (via the link in the confirmation-email): possible up to 6h prior to the reservation time. please contact us by email or phone after that.

    • on the day of your visit, we will hold your table for 20 minutes before offering it to other guests.

    SEATING AREAS
