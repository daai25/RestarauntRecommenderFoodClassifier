Skihütte Genepi

Do you fancy taking a breather and having a little snack or an aperitif? There is always a good buzz in our Genepi ski hut. It is also perfect for private parties. In winter, there is always something going on in or around the venue. It is easy to reach for non-skiers too. A special highlight is live music on Saturdays. After strenuous hiking in summer, you are very welcome to relax in our lounge or on our reclining chairs.

Groups and banquets

All rooms are also available for group reservations. Are you planning a company outing or a group trip? Get in touch with us. We will be happy to put together an individual offer for you.
Groups wishing to visit us outside regular opening hours can either stay overnight directly at the Berghotel or book an extra trip with the Grindelwald-First mountain railroad to take the gondola back to Grindelwald after dinner.

 

Please contact us by phone if you would like to make a restaurant reservation. During the winter season, we take reservations for the lunchtime restaurant service up until 10.00 a.m. at the latest.
