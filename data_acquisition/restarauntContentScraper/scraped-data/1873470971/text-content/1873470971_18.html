Grindelwald First mountain hotel

Stay on Grindelwald’s local mountain

Enjoy magical moments in the tranquillity and seclusion of the surrounding mountain environment. If you are going skiing or hiking in the mountains, why not combine it with a stay in our Grindelwald First mountain inn. We have rooms suitable for individuals, families and groups.

Double room

Enjoy cosy togetherness in the mountains. After a good meal in our mountain inn, you will find that our double room has everything you need for a restful stay on the mountain. Well-rested and fortified after a hearty breakfast, you can start the next day right here in the excellent excursion area of Grindelwald First. Extensive hiking trails beckon in summer, and in winter, the ski slopes begin right outside the door.

  • Beds with cosy duvets
  • Shared showers and WC

Family room

While the children discover the nature around the mountain guesthouse as a playground, the parents enjoy the view from our panoramic terrace with an aperitif. Alpine gem hut – our multifaceted jewel

Our Gemshütte is located slightly below our sun terrace. Originally a small chapel, we also convert it into a dormitory for 15 people. Through the large windows you have an unforgettable view of the imposing surrounding mountains when you first wake up in the morning. A night you will never forget.

Are you looking for a suitable location for your wedding or christening? Our Gemshütte is a small chapel in which we have already celebrated several fantastically romantic mountain weddings. We would be happy to put together an individual offer for you.

How do I get to the hotel?

You can only reach our mountain inn by the Firstbahn gondola lift. Transport is not included in the price of your room. You will find the operating schedule for the gondola cableway at www.jungfrau.ch. It is a trip of about 20 minutes on the gondola and you can be here, enjoying the beautiful mountains.

If you come to Grindelwald by car, we recommend that you park in the Pfingstegg long-term car park. It is about 7 minutes’ walk from the Firstbahn.

If you arrive by train, it is a walk of 15 minutes or so from the train station to the Firstbahn, through the beautiful glacier village of Grindelwald.
